M.11830 - UNICREDIT / BANCO BPM

                         SECTION 1.2

                           Description of the concentration


1)     This notification concerns the acquisition by UniCredit of sole control over BPM
       within the meaning of Article 3(1), point (b) of the Merger Regulation.

2)     UniCredit is an Italian joint stock company (società per azioni), the shares of which
       are listed on regulated stock exchanges in Milan, Frankfurt and Warsaw, and is the
       ultimate controller of the UniCredit Group. The UniCredit Group is an Italian banking
      group providing banking and financial services in Italy and worldwide to over 15
       million retail, commercial, private banking, wealth management and family office
        clients.

3)   BPM is an Italian joint stock company (società per azioni), the shares of which are
        listed on the Milan stock exchange, and is the ultimate controller of the BPM Group.
      The BPM Group’s business activities include the provision of banking and financial
        services, investment and  life insurance solutions, foreign exchange and securities
        trading, and assets management services.

4)    The Proposed Transaction will be carried out by means of the launch by UniCredit of
       a voluntary public exchange offer pursuant to and for the purposes of Article 102 and
       Article 106(4) TUF, in respect of all of the ordinary shares of BPM.
