M.11143 - CMA CGM / BOLLORE LOGISTICS

                         SECTION 1.2

                           Description of the concentration

On 5 January 2024, CMACGM S.A. (“CMA CGM”), a French company whose headquarters
are in Marseille, notified to the Commission a proposed concentration pursuant to Article 4 of
Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004, whereby it intends to acquire, within the meaning of
Article3(1)(b) of the Council Regulation, sole control over Bolloré Logistics SE(“Bolloré
Logistics”), a French-based company, via a share purchase deal.

CMA CGM is active at world level in container liner shipping and port terminal services.
Through its subsidiary CEVA Logistics S.A. (“CEVALogistics”), CMA CGM is also active
in freight forwarding and contract logistics services. In the EEA, CEVA Logistics is active in
Austria, Belgium, Croatia, the Czech Republic, Finland, France, Germany, Greece, Hungary,
Ireland, Italy, Latvia, Lithuania, Luxembourg, the Netherlands, Poland, Portugal, Romania,
Slovakia, Spain, and Sweden.

Bolloré Logistics  is active in freight forwarding and contract logistics services. Bolloré
Logistics operates in particular in Europe (in France as well as in Austria, Belgium, the Czech
Republic,  Denmark,  Finland,  Germany,  Hungary,  Ireland,   Italy,  Luxembourg,  the
Netherlands, Poland, Portugal, Romania, Spain and Sweden).
