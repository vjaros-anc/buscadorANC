M.10806 – BROADCOM / VMWARE

                         SECTION 1.2

                           Description of the concentration


On November 15, 2022, the Commission received notification of a proposed acquisition by
Broadcom Inc. (“Broadcom”) of sole control of VMware, Inc. (“VMware”) (the “Transaction”).
The Transaction constitutes a concentration pursuant to Article 3(1)(b) of Council Regulation
(EC) No 139/2004.31

Broadcom, headquartered in San José, California, United States, is a technology company that
designs, manufactures, and provides a broad range of semiconductors and infrastructure software
solutions. Additional information on Broadcom is available at www.broadcom.com.

VMware, headquartered in Palo Alto, California, United States, is active in IT software including
virtualization and  related workload management  technologies  for  data  centres and cloud
computing  environments,  application  development, and  endpoint management.  Additional
information on VMware is available at www.vmware.com.
