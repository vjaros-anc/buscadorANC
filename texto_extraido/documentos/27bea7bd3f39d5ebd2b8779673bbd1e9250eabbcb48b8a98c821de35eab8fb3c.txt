                MONITORING TRUSTEE IN CASE

      M.12278 – PARAMOUNT SKYDANCE / WARNER BROS DISCOVERY



On 28 August 2026 the Commission approved the appointment of:

             Monitoring Trustee Partners B.V.
            Koninginneweg 215
            1075 CS Amsterdam
            The Netherlands

              Contact:

              Jan Jaap Snel, Managing Director, janjaapsnel@mtp.international
              Astrid Bellon, Director, astridbellon@mtp.international


as Monitoring Trustee in the above-mentioned case.



The role of the Monitoring Trustee is to monitor the compliance of Paramount Skydance
Corporation and Warner Bros. Discovery,  Inc. with the Commitments attached  to the
Commission’s Decision of 22 July 2026 and to report to the Commission thereon.

Third parties who have queries in relation to the Commitments may contact the Trustee through
the above contact details.

The  foregoing  is  without  prejudice  to  the Commission’s  jurisdiction  to  oversee  the
implementation of the Commitments attached to its Decision.
