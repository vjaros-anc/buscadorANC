 MONITORING TRUSTEE IN CASE M.10108 – S&P GLOBAL / IHS MARKIT



On 29.10.2021 the Commission approved the appointment of:

                 Monitoring Trustee Partner B.V. (“MTP”)

                 Address: Koninginneweg 215-H, 1075 CS Amsterdam

                  janjaapsnel@mtp.international, + 31 646 424 992



as Monitoring Trustee in the above-mentioned case.



The role of the Monitoring Trustee is to monitor the compliance of S&P GLOBAL / IHS
MARKIT with the Commitments attached to the Commission's Decision of 22.10.2021
and to report to the Commission thereon.


Third parties who have queries in relation to the Commitments may contact the Trustee
through the above contact details.

The foregoing  is without prejudice to the Commission's jurisdiction to oversee the
implementation of the Commitments attached to its Decision.
