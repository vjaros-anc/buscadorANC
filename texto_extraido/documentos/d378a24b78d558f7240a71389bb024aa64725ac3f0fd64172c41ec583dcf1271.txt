M.10687-D’IETEREN / PHE

                         SECTION 1.2

                           Description of the concentration

On June 13th, 2022, the European Commission received a  notification of a proposed
concentration pursuant to Article 4 of the Merger Regulation by which the D’Ieteren Group
(“DIG”) intends to acquire sole control, within the meaning of Article 3(1)(b) of the Merger
Regulation, of Parts Holding Europe (“PHE”) by acquisition of shares.

DIG, through its portfolio companies, is active in various sectors, in particular (i) import,
distribution and retail of Volkswagen group vehicles and spare parts (Volkswagen, Audi,
Seat, Skoda, Bentley, Lamborghini, Porsche and Bugatti) in Belgium (D’Ieteren Automotive)
and   (ii)  vehicle  glass  repair  and  replacement  services  in  various  member  States
(Belron/Carglass).

PHE is a company incorporated in France and mainly active in spare parts distribution for
light and heavy vehicles operating in the independent aftermarket (“IAM”) in several EU
Member States. In France, in addition to the distribution of spare parts, PHE provides vehicle
glass  repair and  replacement  services.  In Belgium, PHE  distributes  spare  parts and
participates in the development of multi-brand and independent repair garages.
