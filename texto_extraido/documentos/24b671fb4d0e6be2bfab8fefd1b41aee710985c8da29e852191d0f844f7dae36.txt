M.12330 - KIMBERLY-CLARK / KENVUE

                         SECTION 1.2

                           Description of the concentration


1.    This notification concerns the proposed acquisition by Kimberly-Clark Corporation
      (together with its subsidiaries, “Kimberly-Clark”) of sole control over Kenvue Inc.
      (together with its subsidiaries, “Kenvue”) within the meaning of Article 3(1)(b) of
      the Merger Regulation (“Proposed Transaction”). The Proposed Transaction will
     be implemented through an acquisition of all outstanding shares of Kenvue Inc.’s
    common stock pursuant to an agreement dated 2 November 2025.

2.    Kimberly-Clark  Corporation  is  a  US-based  corporation  that  is  the  ultimate
      controlling entity of Kimberly-Clark. Kimberly-Clark is active globally and produces
     and sells a range of products made from natural or synthetic fibers and materials
     using advanced technologies in fibers, nonwovens and absorbency for personal,
      business, and industrial use. Its business is split into two units: (i) International
     Personal Care; and  (ii) North America (which comprises both Family Care &
      Professional, and Personal Care, in that region).

3.   Kenvue Inc. is a US-based corporation that is the ultimate controlling entity of
     Kenvue. Kenvue manufactures and supplies globally consumer health products in the
     following categories: (1) Self-Care (2) Skin Health and Beauty, and (3) Essential
      Health.
