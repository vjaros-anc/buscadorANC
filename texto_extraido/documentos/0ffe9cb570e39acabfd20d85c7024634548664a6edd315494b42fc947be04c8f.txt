M.11481 – SYNOPSYS / ANSYS

                         SECTION 1.2

                           Description of the concentration


On November 11, 2024, the Commission received notification of a proposed acquisition by
Synopsys Inc., (“Synopsys”) of sole control of ANSYS, Inc., (“Ansys”) (the “Transaction”).
The Transaction will be implemented pursuant to a merger agreement concluded on January
15, 2024, by which Synopsys will acquire all of the outstanding shares of common stock of
Ansys through a reverse subsidiary merger. The Transaction constitutes a concentration
pursuant to Article 3(1)(b) of Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004.

Synopsys, headquartered in Sunnyvale, California, United States, is a global provider of
electronic design automation software, tools and services and semiconductor intellectual
property  products  and  services.  Additional  information on  Synopsys  is  available  at
www.synopsys.com.

Ansys, headquartered in Canonsburg, Pennsylvania, United States, is a global provider of
engineering simulation software and services, supplying a wide range of simulation solutions
that allow for the design, testing, and optimization of products and processes across various
industries. Additional information on Ansys is available at www.ansys.com.
