2.     ASSESSMENT OF THE PROPOSAL

2.1.    Legal framework

(6)     Pursuant to paragraph 19 of the Commitments, the Commission has to verify that
         the purchaser fulfils the purchaser criteria and that the Divestment Business is
        being sold in a manner consistent with the Commitments.

(7)     According to paragraph 17 of the Commitments, in its assessment of the proposed
         purchaser, the Commission should ensure in particular that:


          (a)  The Purchaser shall be independent of and unconnected to Sika and  its
               Affiliated Undertakings (this being assessed having regard to the situation
              following the divestiture);


         (b)  The Purchaser  shall have the  financial resources, proven expertise and
              incentive to maintain and develop the Divestment Business as a viable and
               active  competitive  force  in  competition  with  the  Parties  and  other
              competitors;


          (c)  The Purchaser must not be active in downstream industries into which
             chemical admixtures are supplied, and in particular, must not be active
            downstream in the production and supply of cement and/or concrete;

2.3.6.   Absence of prima facie competition concerns

(40)    According to Section D, paragraph 17I of the Commitments, the acquisition of the
        Divestment Business by the Purchaser must neither be likely to create, in light of
         the information available to the Commission, prima facie competition concerns nor
        give rise to a risk that the implementation of the Commitments will be delayed.

(41)    There are no horizontal overlaps or vertical relationships between the activities of
       any of Cinven’s portfolio companies and the Divestment Business given that
       Cinven does not hold any company that is active in the market for chemical
        admixtures or any other market where the Divestment Business is active, nor
        upstream or downstream of such markets.

(42)    The Commission  therefore  considers  that  the  acquisition  of  the Divestment
        Business by Cinven would not be likely to create prima facie competition concerns
         (nor a fortiori give rise to a risk that the implementation of the Commission will be
         delayed).41 In particular, it can be reasonably expected that Cinven will obtain all
        necessary approvals from the relevant regulatory authorities for the acquisition of
         the Divestment Business.

(43)    This prima  facie  assessment  is  based on  the  information  available  to  the
       Commission for the purpose of this decision and does not prejudge the competition
        assessment  of  the  acquisition  of  the Divestment Business by Cinven by a
        competent competition authority under applicable merger control rules. According
         to the notifying party, the acquisition of the Divestment Business will have to be
         notified in the US and Canada.42

2.3.7.   Conclusion on the Purchaser Criteria

(44)     In light of the above considerations and taking into account the Reasoned Opinion
        submitted by the Trustee, the Commission concludes  that Cinven meets the
        Purchaser Criteria set out in paragraph 17 of the Commitments.

2.4.     Sale in a manner consistent with the Commitments

(45)    The Proposed Agreements consist of a […] and a Share Purchase Agreement in
        agreed form and its exhibits, including ancillary agreements.

(46)    The Trustee considers that the assets that are listed as included and excluded in the
        scope of the business to be sold under the Proposed Agreements are in line with the
         provisions of the Commitments.43 In particular, the Proposed Agreements cover the
       worldwide EBA business of MBCC Group and the EBC business of MBCC Group
         in Australia and New Zealand, including all relevant productions sites (including
        warehouses and offices), all R&D facilities (including the Treviso and Trostberg
     R&D sites), all other tangible and intangible assets envisaged in the Commitments,
         as well as all personnel including key personnel.

(47)    The  Trustee  therefore  considers  that  the  full  perimeter  as  set  out  in  the
       Commitments will be divested pursuant to the Proposed Agreements.44

(48)     In addition, the Trustee has not identified any issue in the Proposed Agreements
       which would hinder the Purchaser’s ability to maintain and grow the Divestment
        Business as a competitive force or would be inconsistent with the Commitments.45

(49)    The Trustee also considers that the Transitional Service Agreements,46 to be
        entered into by Sika and the Divestment Business in relation to IT, Finance, HR,
          fleet management, leasing etc., do not raise concerns.47

(50)     In  light of the above considerations and taking into account the information
        provided by Sika and  the Reasoned Opinion submitted by the Trustee, the
       Commission therefore considers that the Proposed Agreements are consistent with
         the Commitments and that, accordingly, the Divestment Business is being sold to
       Cinven in a manner consistent with the Commitments.
