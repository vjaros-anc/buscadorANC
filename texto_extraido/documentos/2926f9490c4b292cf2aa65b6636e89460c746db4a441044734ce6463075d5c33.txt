M.10663 ORANGE / VOO / BRUTELE

                         SECTION 1.2

                          Description of the concentration


On 22 June 2022, the European Commission received a notification concerning the
acquisition of sole control of VOO SA (“VOO”) and the Telecommunications, Media and
Technology (“TMT”) business of Brutélé SC (“Brutélé”) by Orange S.A. (“Orange”).

Following the transaction, Orange will indirectly hold 75% minus one share of VOO’s issued
share capital, itself owning the TMT business of Brutélé.

Orange is a French global telecommunications operator, active in Belgium primarily through
Orange Belgium S.A. (“OBE”) and marginally through other subsidiaries (Orange Business
Belgium, Business & Decision, Orange Cyberdefense and Orange Bank via its subsidiary
Anytime (all these entities referred as “Orange Group Belgium”)). Orange Group Belgium
primarily offers mobile telecommunications services based on its own mobile network. To a
much lesser extent, Orange Group Belgium provides fixed telecommunications  services based
on third party fixed networks.

VOO is a cable operator mainly active in the Walloon Region. VOO primarily provides fixed
telecommunications  services through its own cable network and, to a very limited degree,
mobile telecommunications  services based on third party mobile networks.

Brutélé is a cable operator mainly active in the Brussels-Capital Region and in certain
municipalities  in the Walloon Region (essentially around the city of Charleroi in the province
of Hainaut, in the province of Namur and in the Walloon Brabant province). Brutélé provides
fixed telecommunications services through  its own cable network
