1.    FACTS AND PROCEDURE
(1)    By decision of 29 January 2025 (the ‘Decision’) based on Article 6(1)(b) in
        connection with Article 6(2) of Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004 (the
        ‘Merger Regulation’) (1), the Commission declared the operation by which
        Constantia Flexibles GmbH (‘Constantia’ or the ‘Notifying Party’, Austria)
        acquired within the meaning of Article 3(1)(b) of the Merger Regulation sole
         control of the whole of Aluflexpack AG (‘AFP’ or the ‘Target’, Switzerland)
         (together the ‘Parties’), compatible with the internal market, subject to conditions
       and obligations (the ‘Commitments’).
(2)     The Commitments consist of the divestment of AFP’s entire business of retortable
       aluminium containers and lids for wet pet food and for human food in the EEA.
        This removes the complete overlap between the Parties’ activities in these two
        markets. In addition, several other products supplied by the Omial Plant will be

(10)   MTX is a Europe-based holding company active in more than 40 countries. MTX

        while these products represent  that vast majority, more than 80%, of the

        supply of pre-treated foil in the EEA is a fragmented space with numerous large

(35)   As to the vertical links, the Notifying Party submits that there are no actual vertical
         links between MTX and  the Divestment  Business. No  potential  vertical
         relationship can give rise to competitive concerns either since the supply and
       demand for pre-treated foil is a fragmented and competitive space with numerous
         large players offering. (25)

         that a small share of the Divestment Business sales is concerned by a horizontal
        overlap with MTX  (i.e., less than 20% of the sales). Moreover, the Trustee
         indicates that for  all these horizontal overlaps  (i.e., the supply of flexible

        use applications) the combined market share of the Divestment Business and
