M.10699 - SALMAR / NTS


                         SECTION 1.2

                           Description of the concentration

The notified concentration  relates to the acquisition of control, within the meaning of
Article 3 of the EU Merger Regulation (“EUMR”), by SalMar ASA (“SalMar”) of NTS ASA
(“NTS”). The SalMar Group has farming operations in Central Norway, Northern Norway,
Iceland and in Scotland. SalMar  is ultimately controlled by the Kvarv Group through
Kverva AS (“Kverva”). Kverva is an investment company specializing in the aquaculture and
seafood industry. NTS’ activity consists of fish farming in Norway and Iceland, as well as the
provision of time charters for wellboats, service boats and sea transport vessels.
