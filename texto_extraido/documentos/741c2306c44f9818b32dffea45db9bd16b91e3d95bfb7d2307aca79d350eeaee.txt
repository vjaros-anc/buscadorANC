1.     FACTS AND PROCEDURE

(1)    By decision of 13 February 2024 (the ‘Decision’) based on Article 8(2) of Council
        Regulation (EC) No 139/2004,1 the Commission declared the concentration by
       which Korean Air Lines Co. Ltd (‘Korean Air’, Republic of Korea, referred to
         hereinafter as ‘South Korea’), controlled by Hanjin KAL Co., Ltd, acquires within
         the meaning of Article 3(1)(b) of the Merger Regulation sole control of the whole
         of Asiana Airlines Inc. (‘Asiana’, South Korea), by way of purchase of shares,
        compatible  with  the  internal  market  and  the EEA  Agreement,  subject  to
        compliance with conditions and  obligations annexed  to  the Decision. These

2.     ASSESSMENT OF THE FULFILMENT OF THE COMPLETION CONDITION

(7)     The Completion Condition requirements  set in paragraph 5 of the Passenger
       Commitments are based on compliance of the Passenger Remedy Taker with the
        Entry Schedule annexed to the Passenger Commitments, both in relation to the
         services the Passenger Remedy Taker operated on the Remedy Routes in the past
       and in relation to tickets sold by the Passenger Remedy Taker for future operations
       on the Remedy Routes.

(8)    On 19 June 2024, following a Reasoned Request by Korean Air, the Commission
        decided, in accordance with clause 91 of the Commitments, to extend the entry date
       on the ICN-CDG route set in the Entry Schedule by nine weeks, until 28 August
        2024, on which day entry shall occur with four weekly frequencies (‘the Extension
         Decision’). This extension should be taken into account when applying to the ICN-
     CDG route the requirement in paragraph 5(a) that the services on each Remedy
        Route, including the ICN-CDG route, should be operated “as of the month foreseen
         in the Entry Schedule for the respective route […]”.

(9)    As a result, the Entry Schedule, as revised by the Extension Decision, sets the
        following schedule for entry and increase of weekly frequencies by the Passenger
      Remedy Taker on the Remedy Routes for IATA Summer Season 2024:

 Table 1: The Entry Schedule for the IATA Summer Season 2024 (number of weekly
frequencies per route), as revised by the Extension Decision.

         ICN-CDG            4                4                5
         ICN-FCO            3                3                3
         ICN-BCN                   -                3                3
         ICN-FRA                   -                         -                3

(10)    Below, the Commission will assess the fulfilment of the Completion Condition as
          set in paragraphs 5(a) and 5(b) of the Passenger Commitments.

2.1.     Fulfilment  of  the  requirements  in  paragraph  5(a)  of  the  Passenger
      Commitments

(11)    The Passenger Remedy Taker, T’way, commenced operations of three weekly
         services on the ICN-FRA route on 3 October 2024. Therefore, on 30 October 2024,
       T’way completed four weeks of operation on the route.7

(12)   As a result, in order to fulfil the requirements set in paragraph 5(a) of the Passenger
       Commitments, T’way should have used the Slots on each of the Remedy Routes
         for at least 80% of the time during the period between the start of operations on
        each Remedy Route respectively and 30 October 2024.

(13)     In accordance with the Entry Schedule, T’way commenced operations of three
       weekly services on the ICN-FCO route on 8 August 2024, of four weekly services
       on the ICN-CDG route on 28 August 2024, of three weekly services on the ICN-
     BCN route on 11 September 2024, and, as mentioned above, of three weekly
         services on the ICN-FRA route on 3 October 2024. On 6 October T’way increased
           its services on the ICN-CDG route to five weekly services.

(14)    The Monitoring Trustee confirms that T’way’s commencement of operations and
         increase of frequencies on all the Remedy Routes were in accordance with the
        Frequencies envisaged under the Entry Schedule.

(15)    The Monitoring Trustee further confirms that T’way has reported no cancellations
         of  its services on any of the Remedy Routes and therefore has satisfied the
        requirement to operate the Slots on each of the Remedy Routes for at least 80% of
         the time during the period between the start of operations on each Remedy Route
       and 30 October 2024.

(16)    Therefore, based on the information available to it, and in particular the Monitoring
         Trustee’s report, the Commission considers that the requirements set in paragraph
         5(a) of the Passenger Commitments have been fulfilled.

2.2.     Fulfilment  of  the  requirements  in  paragraph  5(b)  of  the  Passenger
      Commitments

(17)   As confirmed by the Monitoring Trustee, in advance of its entry on the Remedy
        Routes, T’way started selling tickets for the IATA Summer season 2024. By 24
         July 2024, T’way sold tickets on all four Remedy Routes, in accordance with its
        planned entry on each of the routes. On 14 August 2024, T’way confirmed to the
        Monitoring Trustee that it had started selling tickets on all four remedy routes also
         for the IATA Winter Season 2024/2025, and on 26 September 2024 T’way placed
       on sale tickets for the increased frequencies envisaged in the Entry Schedule. On
       25 October 2024, T'way started selling tickets on all four Remedy Routes for the
        frequencies envisaged in the Entry Schedule for the IATA Summer Season 2025,
        including […].

(18)    Therefore, on 25 October 2024 and until the end of the IATA Summer Season
        2024, T'way was selling tickets for the frequencies envisaged under the Entry
        Schedule in relation to all Remedy Routes for at least the ongoing IATA Summer
        Season 2024, the IATA Winter Season 2024/2025 and the IATA Summer Season
        2025.

(19)    Consequently, based on the information available to  it, and in particular the
        Monitoring Trustee’s report, the Commission considers that the requirements set in
        paragraph 5(b) of the Passenger Commitments have been fulfilled.

3.     ADDITIONAL CONSIDERATIONS

(20)   On 31 October 2024, the Commission received a letter from Asiana Airlines Pilots
       Union (‘APU’), requesting, among others, that “during its review process for final
        approval of the business combination between Korean Air and Asiana Airlines” the
       Commission verifies certain facts related to the support provided by Korean Air to
       T’way. In its letter, APU submits that “Korean Air plans to provide additional
        support to T'way Air with two B777 aircraft and personnel including pilots and




                                                     4

[PÁGINA PDF 6]
        maintenance staff”. APU further submits that the additional support is required
        because “T'way Air has recently experienced difficulties in introducing previously
        planned A330 aircraft, causing disruptions in stable operations on European
         routes”, and that “[t]his underlines T'way Air's lack of capability and experience in
        operating major European routes”. APU therefore “request the EC to carefully
       examine this aspect before making its final decision”.

(21)   As explained above at recitals 3 and 4, the Completion Condition sets very specific
        requirements concerning the operation of services on the Remedy Routes and the
         sale of tickets on these routes. As the Completion Condition does not concern the
        circumstances mentioned in the letter from APU, there is no room to take these
        elements into account in the current decision. However, the Commission will
        continue to monitor Korean Air’s compliance with the Passenger Commitments
        including in relation to the circumstances brought to its attention by APU.

4.     CONCLUSION

(22)   On the basis of the above assessment and the information provided to  it, the
       Commission concludes that the Completion Condition set in paragraph 5 of the
        Passenger Commitments has been fulfilled.

(23)    This decision only concerns an assessment of the fulfilment of the Completion
        Condition. It does not constitute a confirmation that Korean Air has complied with
           its Commitments more generally.

(24)    This decision is based on paragraph 5 of the Passenger Commitments attached to
         the Commission Decision of 13 February 2024.
