M.10560 - SIKA / MBCC GROUP

                        SECTION 1.2

                          Description of the concentration

On 12 December 2022, the Commission received a notification of a proposed concentration
pursuant to which Sika AG ("Sika") will, according to a Sale and Purchase Agreement by
and among Sika  International AG and LSF11 Skyscraper Midco 2  S.à.r.l. dated 10
November 2021, indirectly acquire sole control over the MBCC group of companies
("MBCC Group"), by way of acquisition of all shares in LSF11 Skyscraper HoldCo S.à.r.l.

The business activities of the Parties are the following:
− Sika AG is the parent company of the Sika group of companies. Sika develops and
produces high-quality chemical admixtures, mortars, sealants and adhesives, damping and
reinforcing materials, structural strengthening systems, industrial flooring, as well as roofing
and waterproofing systems, which are used in the building sector and by manufacturing
industries.
− MBCC Group's business to be acquired has two strategic business units:
(i)  "chemical  admixtures", which  provides  solutions  for  customers  in  the  concrete
manufacturing, cement and underground construction  industries, and  (ii) "construction
systems", which offers solutions to protect and repair buildings
and structures.
