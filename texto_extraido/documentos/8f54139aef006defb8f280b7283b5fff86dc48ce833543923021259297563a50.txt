M.11383 - COOPER / VIATRIS (EUROPEAN OTC BUSINESS)

                         SECTION 1.2

                           Description of the concentration


    1. On 3 May 2024, the Commission received notification of a proposed concentration
       pursuant  to  Article 4 of Council Regulation (EC) No. 139/2004 involving the
        acquisition of sole control over the European OTC business of Viatris Inc. (the
       "Target") by Cooper Consumer Health S.A.S. ("Cooper") within the meaning of
        Article 3(1)(b) of Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004.

    2. The primary business activities of the undertakings are:

             a.  Cooper is principally active in the manufacturing and distribution of OTC,
            consumer health and consumer self-care products.

            b.  Target is the European OTC business of Viatris. The assets include a range of
               finished  dose  pharmaceuticals  across, amongst  others, body  care, body
               cleansing,  intimate  hygiene,  food  supplements,  homeopathics and baby
              hygiene. The acquired entities provide R&D, manufacturing and operational
                infrastructure support in connection with the assets.
