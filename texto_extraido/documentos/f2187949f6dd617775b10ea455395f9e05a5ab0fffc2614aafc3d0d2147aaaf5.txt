2.1.    Legal framework

(13)    Pursuant to paragraphs 21-22 of the Cargo Commitments, the Commission shall

        Business  is being sold in a manner consistent with the Cargo Commitments.
        Paragraph 21 of the Cargo Commitments provides that in order to be approved by
         the Commission, the Purchaser must fulfil the following criteria:
          (a)  The purchaser shall be independent of and unconnected to Korean Air and its

              following the divestiture);
         (b)  The purchaser  shall have the  financial resources, proven expertise and

               active  competitive  force  in  competition  with  the  Parties  and  other
              competitors;
          (c)  The purchaser shall be a Korean airline with an existing Air Operator’s

          aircraft on routes across Asia, mainly between South Korea and each of China,
        Japan, Mongolia, Singapore and Vietnam. Since its establishment, Air Incheon has

         several certificates and awards by renowned authorities, including by IATA (IATA
        Operational Safety Audit program IOSA) and the United Nations (UN-designated
        cargo airline registration).16

(39)    Furthermore, Air Incheon’s personnel brings significant experience from the airline
         industry. The senior management and directors of Air Incheon have extensive
          airline and/or finance experience, while some were holding key positions in Korean
        Air and/or Asiana in the past. At the same time, many of Air Incheon’s pilots and
          field maintenance personnel are also former employees of Korean Air and/or
        Asiana, meaning they already have the relevant experience needed to operate the
        Divestment Business’s aircraft types. According to Air Incheon, those individuals

2.3.2.3. Incentive

(43)    The Commission considers, based on the main  financial  indicators,  that the

        Incheon, “[t]he merger, including the transfer of  traffic  rights, enhances Air

         diversify  its routes and service offerings […] Air Incheon aims to become a
