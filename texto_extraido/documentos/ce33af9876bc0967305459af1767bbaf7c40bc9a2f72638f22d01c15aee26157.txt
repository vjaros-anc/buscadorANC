   MONITORING TRUSTEE IN CASE M.10431 – ALI GROUP / WELBILT


On 21 June 2022 the Commission approved the appointment of:


                 Monitoring Trustee Partners B.V.
                Koninginneweg 215-H
               1075 CS Amster
               The Netherlands

                  Contact:
                 Jan Jaap Snel
                 JanJaapSnel@MTP.international



as Monitoring Trustee in the above-mentioned case.


The role of the Monitoring Trustee is to monitor the compliance of Ali Group and
Welbilt with the Commitments attached to the Commission's Decision of 17 June 2022
and to report to the Commission thereon.

Third parties who have queries in relation to the Commitments may contact the Trustee
through the above contact details.

The foregoing  is without prejudice to the Commission's jurisdiction to oversee the
implementation of the Commitments attached to its Decision.
