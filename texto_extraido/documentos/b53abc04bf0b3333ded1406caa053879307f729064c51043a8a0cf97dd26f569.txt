               EUROPEAN COMMISSION


                                 Hearing Officer for competition proceedings

            Final Report of the Hearing Officer (1)
          Case M.11956 – UMG / DOWNTOWN

    1. On 16 June 2025, following a referral pursuant to Article 22 of the Merger
       Regulation, (2) the Commission received a notification of a proposed transaction
       pursuant to Article 4 of the Merger Regulation by which Universal Music Group
      N.V.  (the  ‘Notifying  Party’)  intends  to  acquire  within  the  meaning  of
        Article 3(1)(b) of the Merger Regulation sole control of the whole of Downtown
      Music Holdings LLC (together the ‘Parties’).

    2. On 22 July 2025, the Commission adopted a decision initiating proceedings
       pursuant to Article 6(1)(c) of the Merger Regulation and finding that the proposed
        transaction raised serious doubts as to its compatibility with the internal market
      and  with  the  functioning  of  the EEA  Agreement. On 20 August  2025,
       the Notifying Party submitted written comments on the Article 6(1)(c) decision.

    3. On 24 July 2025, the deadline was extended by 10 working days in accordance
       with Article 10(3) second subparagraph, first sentence of the Merger Regulation.

    4. On 3 September 2025, the Commission issued two decisions, on the basis of
        Article 11(3) of the Merger Regulation, to each Party suspending the time limit
        referred to in Article 10(3) of the Merger Regulation from 2 September 2025. The
       time limit resumed on 20 October 2025.

    5. On 24 November 2025, the Commission issued a statement of objections (‘SO’)
       concluding  on  a  preliminary  basis  that  the  proposed  transaction  would
        significantly impede effective competition in a substantial part of the internal
       market.

    6. On 8 December 2025, the Notifying Party submitted written comments  in
       response to the SO. They did not ask for an Oral Hearing.

    7.  Access to file was first provided on 24 November 2025 in connection with the
       issue of the SO. Subsequent access to file was provided on 25 November 2025,
      19 December 2025, 23 December 2025, 30 January 2026 and 4 February 2026.
         I have not received any complaints or requests concerning access to file.

    8.  Throughout the proceedings, I admitted two interested third persons, pursuant to
        Article 5 of Decision 2011/695/EU. DG Competition informed the interested

    9. On 11 December 2025, the Parties submitted a first set of commitments with
       a view  to rendering  the  concentration compatible with  the  internal market
       pursuant to Article 8(2) of the Merger Regulation (the ‘Initial Commitments’).
      The Commission  launched  a  market  test  of  the  Initial Commitments on
      12 December 2025.

    10. The Commission gave the Parties detailed feedback on the outcome of the market
         test on 22 December 2025.

    11. On 15 January 2026, the Parties submitted a revised and final set of commitments
        (the ‘Final Commitments’).

    12. The draft decision declares the proposed transaction compatible with the internal
      market  and  the EEA  Agreement,  subject  to  compliance  with  the  Final
      Commitments.

    13. I have reviewed  the  draft  decision pursuant  to  Article 16(1)  of Decision
      2011/695/EU and I conclude that it deals only with objections in respect of which
       the Parties have been afforded the opportunity of making their views known.

    14. Overall, I consider that the effective exercise of the Parties’ procedural rights has
      been respected.
