Dear Sir or Madam,



1.    FACTS AND PROCEDURE
(1)    By decision of 13 February 2026 (the ‘Decision’) based on Article 8(2) of the
       Merger  Regulation (1),  the Commission  declared  the  operation by which
        Universal Music Group N.V. (‘UMG’ or the ‘Notifying Party’, Netherlands) will
        acquire within the meaning of Article 3(1)(b) of the Merger Regulation sole
         control of the whole of Downtown Music Holdings LLC (‘Downtown’, United
         States of America, together with UMG, the ‘Parties’) (the ‘Transaction’)
        compatible with the internal market following modification by the Parties, subject
         to conditions and obligations (the ‘Commitments’).



(1)      Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004 of 20.1.2004 on the control of concentrations between
         undertakings, OJ L 24, 29.1.2004, p. 1 (the ‘Merger Regulation’).

[PÁGINA PDF 3]
(2)     The Commitments consist in the divestment to a suitable purchaser(s) (the
        ‘Purchaser’) (2) of Downtown’s subsidiary Curve royalty accounting business,
        including Curve’s cloud-based royalty accounting software platform (the ‘Curve
        Platform’), in the form of a legal entity Curve Royalty Systems, Ltd (United
       Kingdom) (the ‘Divestment Business’). (3) The Divestment Business includes
         the transfer of all assets and employees that contribute to the current operation of
       Curve Platform. Recitals 599 and 633 of the Decision contain a detailed
         description of the Divestment Business.
(3)    By letter of 12 June 2026 (‘Reasoned Proposal’), the Parties proposed Jamen
         Capital LLP  (‘Jamen’)  and Music  and  Entertainment  Rights  Licensing
        Independent Network Ltd (‘Merlin’, together with Jamen, the ‘Co-Investors’ or
       ‘Proposed Purchaser’) for approval by the Commission as purchaser of the
        Divestment Business and submitted the proposed share purchase agreement
        signed on 6 June 2026 (‘SPA’ or ‘Proposed Agreement’). (4) On 13 July 2026,
        Monitoring Trustee Partners B.V. (the ‘Trustee’) submitted an assessment of the
        Proposed Purchaser’s suitability as a purchaser and, in particular, indicated that it
          fulfils the criteria of the purchaser requirements in section D of the Commitments
        attached to the Decision (‘Reasoned Opinion’). (5) In this assessment, the
        Trustee also indicated  that, on the basis of the Proposed Agreement, the
        Divestment  Business  would  be  sold  in  a  manner  consistent  with  the
       Commitments.

2.3.    Absence of prima facie competition concerns
(32)    According to paragraph 19(d) of the Commitments, the acquisition of the
        Divestment Business by the Proposed Purchaser must neither be likely to create,
         in light of the information available to the Commission, prima facie competition
        concerns, nor give rise to a risk that the implementation of the Commitments will
        be delayed. In particular, the Proposed Purchaser must reasonably be expected to
        obtain all necessary approvals from the relevant regulatory authorities for the
         acquisition of the Divestment Business.
(33)    Based on the information provided by the Parties, the Proposed Purchaser and the

        Purchaser for the reasons set out below.

2.3.1.   Absence of prima facie competition concerns
(34)    Based on the information provided by the Parties, the Proposed Purchaser and the
         Trustee, the Commission considers that the sale of the Divestment Business to the
        Co-Investors will not give rise to prima facie competition concerns for the
        following reasons.
(35)     First, since neither Jamen nor Merlin have activities in the same market as the
        Divestment Business, the proposed sale of the Divestment Business to the Co-
         Investors does not bring about any horizontal overlap. (40)
(36)    Second, the Parties explain that the Co-Investors do not have upstream or
       downstream activities linked to the Divestment Business. (41) Therefore, the Co-
         Investors would have neither the ability nor the incentive to engage in input or
        customer foreclosure given the absence of upstream and downstream activities
        linked to the Divestment business.
(37)     Third, neither Jamen nor Merlin hold significant complementary market positions
        or portfolios to those of the Divestment Business. (42) In  this regard, the
       Commission recalls that the market share of the Divestment Business in the global
         royalty accounting  services market  is [5-10]%. (43) Regarding Jamen,  the
       Commission notes that since Jamen only started operations in 2025, it currently
        has limited revenues and market position. (44) Regarding Merlin, the Commission
        notes that Merlin’s market position in the artist and label services is below [0-
       5]%. (45) Accordingly, the Commission considers that the market position of the
        Divestment Business and the Proposed Purchaser would not give rise to non-
         horizontally affected relationships.
