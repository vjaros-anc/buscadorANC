1.     FACTS AND PROCEDURE

(1)    By decision of 23 February 2024 (the ‘Decision’) based on Article 6(1)(b) in
        connection with Article 6(2) of Council Regulation No 139/2004,1 the Commission
        declared the operation by which CMA CGM S.A. (‘CMA CGM’ or ‘Notifying
         Party’, France) acquires, by way of purchase of shares, sole control of the whole of
        Bolloré Logistics SE (‘Bolloré Logistics’, France), compatible with the internal
        market, subject to conditions and obligations provided in the Annex of the Decision
         (the ‘Commitments’). CMA CGM and Bolloré Logistics are hereinafter jointly
        designated as the ‘Parties’.

2.     ASSESSMENT OF THE PROPOSAL

2.1.    Legal framework

(11)    According to paragraphs 19-20 of the Commitments, the Commission has to verify
         that the Divestment Business  is being sold in a manner consistent with the
       Commitments and that the purchaser fulfils the purchaser criteria. According to
        paragraph 19 of the Commitments, in its assessment of the proposed purchaser, the
       Commission should ensure that:
          (a)  The purchaser is independent of and unconnected to the Notifying Party and
                   its affiliated undertakings.
         (b)  The purchaser has the financial resources, proven expertise, and incentive to
             maintain and develop the Divestment Business as a  viable and  active
              competitive force in competition with the Parties and other competitors.
          (c)  The acquisition of the Divestment Business by the purchaser is not likely to
                create, in light of the information available to the Commission, prima facie
              competition concerns nor give rise to a risk that the implementation of the
           Commitments will be delayed. In particular, the purchaser must reasonably

(13)    Balguerie is a French freight forwarder offering a comprehensive range of services
         in  the  transport  sector,  including  maritime,  aerial, and  terrestrial  transport.

        United States. Balguerie employs around 900 employees across 25 operational sites
       and 40 locations worldwide.

       prima facie competition problems.

2.4.    Assessment of the Proposed Agreements

(47)    The Proposed Agreements consist of a Shares and Assets Purchase Agreement,
        dated 7 June 2024, covering the sale of the Divestment Business to Balguerie, and
         related agreements.

(48)    The scope of the transaction, as defined in the Proposed Agreements, covers
        Bolloré Logistics’ shares in each of the Divested Entities (including  its assets,
         licenses,  permits,  authorisations,  customers,  personnel,  and  leases).29  The
       Commission notes that the actual number of customers and employees of the
        Divested  Entities  do  not  fully  coincide  with  the  ones  provided  in  the
        Commitments.30 However, the variations are limited and caused by normal course
         of business factors. Moreover, the Trustee confirms that all current employees and
        customers  of  the  Divested  Entities  are  included  in  the  perimeter  of  the
         transaction.31

(49)     In line with the Commitments, the Proposed Agreements also cover the transfer of
         relationships with customers with  activities in Martinique, Guadeloupe, Saint
        Martin, and French Guiana currently managed by Bolloré Logistics France.32 As
         specified in page 3 of the Commitments, a limited number of customers are
        excluded from the scope of the Divestment Business. The lists of Global Excluded
         Clients identified in the Form RM and in the Commitments33 were updated to
        account for sales achieved in 2023 and are currently listed in Schedule 14.6.1 to the
        Shares and Assets Purchase Agreement. The Trustee did not identify missing
         customers.34

(50)     Finally, in line with the Commitments, the Proposed Agreements also cover the
         transfer of a number of personnel of Bolloré Logistics France located in mainland
        France. The Commission notes that there is a limited variation in the number of

         the purchaser automatically, but only on a voluntary basis, due to the applicable
         national labour  legislation),36 Bolloré Logistics will be able  to automatically
         transfer the personnel of Bolloré Logistics France located in mainland France with
         the Divested Entities.37 This modification, by eliminating the risk that some of
