      MONITORING TRUSTEE IN CASE M.10860 – ADVENT/GFK


On 04.07.2023 the Commission approved the appointment of:

                 Monitoring Trustee Partners B.V.
                Koninginneweg 215-H
               1075 CS Amsterdam
               The Netherlands
                        tel. +31 646 424 992
                  email: janjaapsnel@mtp.international


as Monitoring Trustee in the above-mentioned case.


The role of the Monitoring Trustee is to monitor the compliance of Advent with the
Commitments attached to the Commission's Decision of 04.07.2023 and to report to the
Commission thereon.

Third parties who have queries in relation to the Commitments may contact the Trustee
through the above contact details.

The foregoing  is without prejudice to the Commission's jurisdiction to oversee the
implementation of the Commitments attached to its Decision.
