M.11936 - NASPERS / JUST EAT TAKEAWAY

                         SECTION 1.2

                           Description of the concentration


The European Commission (“Commission”)  has  received  notification  of  a proposed
concentration pursuant to Article 4 of the Merger Regulation by which MIH BidCo, an
indirect wholly owned subsidiary of Prosus N.V. (“Prosus”), intends to acquire within the
meaning of Article 3(1)(b) of the Merger Regulation sole control over Just Eat Takeaway.com
N.V. (“JET”). The concentration will be implemented through the launch of a public bid
which may result in Prosus controlling all, but in any event more than 67%, of the shares in
JET.

Prosus is a globally active consumer internet group and technology investor headquartered in
Amsterdam, Netherlands and listed on Euronext Amsterdam. Prosus is controlled by Naspers
Limited, a multinational holding company headquartered in Cape Town, South Africa.
Prosus’ portfolio comprises companies active in food delivery, eCommerce, fintech, classified
advertising and edtech across the EEA and worldwide. Naspers, through Prosus, owns a
minority shareholding in Delivery Hero SE.

JET is a global on-demand delivery company, connecting consumers and partners through its
online food ordering and delivery platforms. JET operates platforms that enable consumers to
order food, predominantly from local restaurants, via websites and mobile applications. The
company is headquartered in Amsterdam, the Netherlands and its shares are listed on the
Euronext Amsterdam exchange.

JET is active in Austria, Belgium, Bulgaria, Denmark, Germany, Ireland, Italy, Luxembourg,
the Netherlands, Poland, Slovakia and Spain in the EEA as well as Australia, Canada, Israel,
Switzerland and the UK.

Neither Naspers nor JET is currently controlled by anyone or any company.
