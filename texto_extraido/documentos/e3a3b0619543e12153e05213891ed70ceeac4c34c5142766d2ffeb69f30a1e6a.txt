M.11204 – BUNGE / VITERRA

                         SECTION 1.2

                           Description of the concentration

(1)     This  notification  relates  to a  concentration pursuant  to  Article 4  of Council
        Regulation (EC) 139/2004 by which Bunge Global SA (“Bunge”) will acquire sole
         control within the meaning of Article 3(1)(b) of Council Regulation (EC) 139/2004
        over Viterra Limited (“Viterra”), by way of a purchase of all shares in Viterra.

(2)     Bunge is an agribusiness company listed on the New York Stock Exchange and
        headquartered in St. Louis, Missouri. Bunge is mainly active in the sale of oilseed
        meals and oils as well as other vegetable oils. Bunge also sells grains, including corn,
        wheat, barley and milled products, such as wheat flour, as well as unprocessed
         oilseeds and other products like sugar.

(3)      Viterra  is an  agribusiness company  that  is  headquartered  in  Rotterdam,  the
        Netherlands. Viterra is currently owned by Glencore, CPP Investments, BCI, and the
      VEBT. Viterra focuses on the purchase and sale of unprocessed commodity crops, in
         particular grains and oilseeds. Viterra also sells other unprocessed products like
        cotton and sugar, as well as milled products (e.g., wheat flour) and oilseed-based
        products (e.g., meal and oil).
