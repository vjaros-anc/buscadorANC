M.11564 - INTERNATIONAL PAPER / DS SMITH

                         SECTION 1.2

                           Description of the concentration


The Proposed Transaction concerns the acquisition of sole control of DSS by IP within the
meaning of Art. 3(1)(b) of Council regulation (EC) No 139/2004 in an all-share transaction.

International Paper, whose shares are traded on the New York Stock Exchange, is a provider
of renewable fibre-based packaging and pulp products, and a recycler of fibrebased waste.
Headquartered in Memphis, Tennessee, the company serves customers from manufacturing
operations in North America, Latin America, North Africa, and Europe.

DSS is a public company incorporated in the United Kingdom, whose shares are traded on the
main market of the London Stock Exchange. It is an international provider of sustainable
fibre-based packaging mainly in Europe and North America which is supported by recycling
and paper-making operations.
