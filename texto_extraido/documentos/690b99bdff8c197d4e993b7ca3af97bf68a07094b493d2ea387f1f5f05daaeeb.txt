M.10575 - BOUYGUES / EQUANS


                         SECTION 1.2

                           Description of the concentration

On 30 May 2022, the European Commission received notification of a proposed concentration
pursuant to Article 4 of Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004 whereby Bouygues S.A.
(“Bouygues”) intends to acquire sole control of Equans S.A.S. (“Equans”), which is currently
controlled by ENGIE S.A. (“ENGIE”).

The business activities of the undertakings concerned are:

  Bouygues: construction, transport infrastructure construction and maintenance, real estate
   development, media and telecommunications;

  Equans: multi-technical services, including electrical, mechanical, and heat, ventilation
   and air-conditioning (“HVAC”) services, refrigeration solutions, facility management,
    digitization and Information and Communication Technology (“ICT”).
