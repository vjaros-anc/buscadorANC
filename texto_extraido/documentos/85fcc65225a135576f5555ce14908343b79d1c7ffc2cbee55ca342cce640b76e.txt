     MONITORING TRUSTEE IN CASE M.10341 – PRINCE / FERRO


On 7.02.2022 the Commission approved the appointment of:

          S&W Partners LLP (formerly known as Smith & Williamson LLP)

                 Address: 45 Gresham Street, London EC2V 7BG, United Kingdom

                Nasoul Gopal, Partner & Head of Monitoring Trustee Services,
                 Email: nasoul.gopal@swgroup.com
                 Phone: +44 (0) 20 4617 5423

as Monitoring Trustee in the above-mentioned case.


The role of the Monitoring Trustee is to monitor the compliance of Prince / Ferro with
the Commitments attached to the Commission's Decision of 25/01/2022 and to report to
the Commission thereon.

Third parties who have queries in relation to the Commitments may contact the Trustee
through the above contact details.

The foregoing  is without prejudice to the Commission's jurisdiction to oversee the
implementation of the Commitments attached to its Decision.
