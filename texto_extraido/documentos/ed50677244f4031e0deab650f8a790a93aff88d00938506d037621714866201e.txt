M.12201 - HOLCIM / XELLA

                         SECTION 1.2

                           Description of the concentration

The concentration concerns the proposed indirect acquisition of sole control within the
meaning of Article 3(1)(b) of the EU Merger Regulation by Holcim Ltd. (together with its
subsidiaries, “Holcim”) over Xella International S.A. (together with its subsidiaries, “Xella”,
and  together  with  Holcim,  the  “Parties”) from LSF10 XL MIDCO S.À  R.L.  (the
“Transaction”).

Holcim specialises in the manufacturing and wholesale supply of building materials, with a
wide range of products including cement, aggregates, ready mix concrete, as well as a variety
of building solutions (mortars, tile adhesives, and facades to coatings, insulations etc.).

Xella develops, produces, and sells building materials and insulating materials – based on
mineral  resources.  Its main products  are divided  into four big  categories, namely  (i)
autoclaved aerated concrete,  (ii) calcium silicate units,  (iii) insulation solutions, and (iv)
autoclaved aerated concrete panels (large format  units) for mainly  residential but also
commercial and industrial construction. Pre-Transaction, Xella is indirectly wholly owned
and controlled by Lone Star Funds, a globally active investment firm with a strong focus on
private equity, credit and real estate.

The Transaction will accelerate Holcim's NextGen Growth 2030 strategy by expanding
Holcim’s Building  Solutions  business through  the  addition  of  Xella’s complementary
sustainable masonry products. Xella's focus on energy-efficient repair and refurbishment,
modular  and  circular  construction,  and  digital  automation  will  strengthen  Holcim's
sustainability commitment. The integration of the complementary product portfolios of the
two companies will enhance the combined group's ability to deliver cost-effective, sustainable
building solutions to customers.
