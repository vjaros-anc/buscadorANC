The role of the Monitoring Trustee is to monitor the compliance of The Boeing Company
and Spirit AeroSystems, Inc. with the Commitments attached to the Commission's
Decision of 14 October 2025 and to report to the Commission thereon.

Third parties who have queries in relation to the Commitments may contact the Trustee
through the above contact details.

implementation of the Commitments attached to its Decision.
