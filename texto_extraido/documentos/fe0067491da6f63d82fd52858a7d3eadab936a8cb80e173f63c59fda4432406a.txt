M.11956 – UMG / DOWNTOWN

                         SECTION 1.2

                           Description of the concentration


This notification concerns the proposed acquisition by Universal Music Group N.V. (UMG)
of sole control over Downtown Music Holdings LLC (Downtown) within the meaning of
Article 3(1), point (b) EUMR.

The concentration  is effected by purchase of shares pursuant to a Membership Interest
Purchasing Agreement entered into by the parties on 16 December 2024.

UMG is a global company incorporated in the Netherlands and listed on Euronext Amsterdam
owning and operating businesses engaged in wholesale recorded music, music publishing,
merchandising, and audio-visual content. On closing, Downtown will be incorporated into
Virgin Music Group, a standalone division within UMG, providing A&L services to third-
party record labels and artists.

Downtown (which is ultimately controlled by Myers Business Trust) is a global independent
rights management and music services company, headquartered in New York, USA, active in
the provision of A&L services, relatively limited publishing services focused on royalty
collection, and certain ancillary services.
