Subject:   Case M.11204 – BUNGE / VITERRA
          Approval of Louis Dreyfus Company as purchaser of the Hungarian
          Divestment Business and the Polish Divestment Business following your
             letter of 3 December 2024 and the Trustee’s opinion of 7 April 2025


Dear Sir or Madam,


1.    FACTS AND PROCEDURE
(1)    By decision of 1 August 2024 (the ‘Decision’) based on Article 6(1)(b) in
        connection with Article 6(2) of Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004 (the
        ‘Merger Regulation’) (1), the Commission declared the acquisition within the
       meaning of Article 3(1)(b) of the Merger Regulation by Bunge Global S.A.
        (‘Bunge’) sole control of Viterra Limited (‘Viterra’, and together with Bunge
         ‘the Parties’) compatible with the internal market following modification by
       Bunge, subject to conditions and obligations (the ‘Commitments’).

(4)     The sale of the Divestment Business to LDC is subject to the Commission’s
        approval. In order to assess whether LDC  is a suitable purchaser for the
        Divestment Business, the Commission verifies that is it independent from the

        maintain and develop the Divested Business as a viable and active competitor,

(48)   On 3 December  2024,  the  Parties  signed two  separate  share  purchase

3.2.    Implementation modalities
(55)    The Commission raised three material issues in relation to the SPAs.
(56)     First, the SPAs as signed on 3 December 2024 were not in line with clause 8 of
         the Commitments  relating  to  the CAPEX  investment  obligation,  which
         stipulates that: “Bunge undertakes to make available to the Purchaser CAPEX
        funding  to fund investment  projects  in Hungary and Poland  specified  in
         Exhibit 1, provided that: (i) the Purchaser demonstrates that the funds will be
        used to fund the investment projects mentioned above; and (ii) the Purchaser
        requests the funds within […] of the date of Closing.” In particular, clause 10.2
        of the Polish SPA and clause 9.2 of the Hungarian SPA provided that the Seller
       would pay the entire CAPEX funding (or ‘CAPEX amount’) to LDC at closing
         (instead of if and when LDC would request it), and LDC would commit to use it
        within […] and only for the purpose of funding projects listed in an exhibit to
         the SPAs. In addition, no monitoring mechanism was put in place to verify that
         the disbursement of the CAPEX amount (or part thereof) would be done in
        compliance with the Commitments.
(57)    Following discussions with the Commission, the SPA Amendment modified
        clause 10.2 of the Polish SPA and clause 9.2 of the Hungarian SPA to provide
         that the Parties will deposit the CAPEX amount in an escrow account at closing,
       and that the CAPEX amount (or parts thereof) would be released to LDC at its
         request (within  the […] from  the completion  date) provided  that  it  first
        demonstrates (in a form acceptable to the Trustee) that the amount to be released
         shall be utilised solely for the purposes of funding capital expenditures in
         respect of the investment projects listed in the SPAs.
(58)    Second, the Polish SPA as signed on 3 December 2024 was not in line with
         clauses 13 and 14 of the Commitments which provides that: “The Parties
        undertake that the Viterra traders in Poland listed in Exhibit 2 that are retained
        by the Parties will not solicit the customers of the Divestment Business for a
        period of […] after Closing.” In particular, the Polish SPA did not include any
         non-solicitation of customers clause.
(59)    Following discussions with the Commission, the SPA Amendment inserted a
      new  clause  in  the  Polish SPA  providing  the  necessary  non-solicitation
         obligations.
(60)    Third, to address the Commission’s concerns regarding the continuity of the
        railway service, which is critical for the Hungarian Divestment Business, Bunge
       committed to include the railway services agreement within the scope of the
         Transitional  Service Agreements  (‘TSA’).  This  is  reflected  in  the SPA
      Amendment via modification of Clause 6.4(a) of the Hungarian SPA which sets
