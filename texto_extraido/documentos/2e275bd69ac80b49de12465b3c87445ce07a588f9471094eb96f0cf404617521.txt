Subject:  Case M.11159 – JD SPORTS / COURIR
         Approval of Snipes SE following your submission of 30 October 2024 and
           the Trustee’s opinion of 7 November 2024

1.     FACTS AND PROCEDURE
(1)    By decision of 22 October 2024 (the ‘Decision’) based on Article 6(1)(b) in
        connection with Article 6(2) of Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004 (the ‘Merger
         Regulation’),1 the Commission declared the operation by which JD Sports Fashion
         plc (‘JD Sports’, UK) acquires within the meaning of Article 3(1)(b) of the Merger
        Regulation sole control of the whole of Groupe Courir SAS (‘Courir’, France) (the
         ‘Transaction’) compatible with the internal market following modification of the
        Transaction by JD Sports and  Courir,  subject  to conditions and  obligations
         (the ‘Commitments’). JD Sports and Courir are jointly referred to as the ‘Parties’.

(2)     The Commitments require that the Parties transfer 21 Courir stores, including 15 in
        France and 6 in Portugal (the ‘Divestment Business’) to a single purchaser,
        Snipes SE (‘Snipes’, Germany).

(3)    On 9 September 2024, and in order to assist the Commission with the assessment
         of the suitability of Snipes as purchaser of the Divestment Business, the Parties
        appointed ALCIS ADVISERS GmbH (‘Alcis’) as Independent Advisor. Alcis was
        assigned to review data from the Parties and Snipes in relation to the Divestment
        Business’  viability and  profitability, and  Snipes’  estimated  market  position
        following the anticipated acquisition of the Courir stores. Alcis was also asked to
         assess Snipes’ independence, financial capabilities, experience and incentives to
        operate the Divestment Business as a viable and active competitive force in
        competition with the Parties and other competitors.

(4)    On 4 October 2024, the Parties submitted an initial reasoned proposal in relation to
        Snipes’ suitability as a purchaser of the Divestment Business (the ‘Initial Reasoned
         Proposal’). The Parties argued in their Initial Reasoned Proposal that Snipes is
        independent  of and unconnected  to  the  Parties, has  the  financial  resources
        necessary to acquire and operate the Divestment Business, and has the proven
         expertise and incentive to maintain and operate the Divestment Business as a viable
       and active competitive force in competition with the Parties and other competitors.
       The Parties further argued that the acquisition by Snipes is unlikely to create prima
         facie competition concerns in France or Portugal.

(5)    On 18 October 2024, Alcis submitted to the Commission a first reasoned opinion
         as to the independence and overall suitability of the proposed purchaser, Snipes
         (the ‘First Reasoned Opinion’). Alcis in its First Reasoned Opinion agreed with the
        submissions of the Parties as to the suitability of Snipes as Purchaser of the
        Divestment Business.

(6)    On 28 October 2024 and following the adoption of the Decision, the Commission
        approved the appointment by the Parties of Alcis as Monitoring Trustee (the
         ‘Trustee’).

(7)    On 30 October 2024, the Parties submitted their final reasoned proposal on the
          suitability of Snipes as purchaser and consistency of the transaction agreements
        entered  into between Courir and Snipes with  the Commitments  (the  ‘Final
       Reasoned  Proposal’). The  Trustee reviewed  the  transaction agreements and
        submitted  its reasoned opinion on 7 November 2024  (the  ‘Final Reasoned
        Opinion’).

2.     ASSESSMENT OF THE PURCHASER’S SUITABILITY
(8)     Snipes is a sports goods retailer, headquartered in Cologne, wholly owned by
       Deichmann SE, a German stock corporation, which was identified by the Parties as
        a competitor on the market for the retail supply of leisure sports footwear and
        apparel in both France and Portugal.

(9)      In the Decision, the Commission considered Snipes as a suitable purchaser of the
        Divestment Business.

(10)     First, in section 6.3.3.1 of the Decision, the Commission concluded that Snipes is
        independent and unconnected to the  Parties. Second, in section 6.3.3.2.1 the
       Commission found that Snipes has sufficient financial resources to complete the
         acquisition of the Divestment Business and to maintain and develop the Divestment
        Business  as a  viable and  active  competitive  force.  Third,  the Commission
        concluded in section 6.3.3.2.2 that Snipes has the proven experience and resources
        necessary to perform a smooth integration process of the Divestment Business
        while maintaining operational continuity in the stores. Fourth, in section 6.3.3.2.3

3.2.    The Commission’s assessment
(20)    The Commission reviewed the Parties’ Final Reasoned Submission in relation to
         the compliance of the Transaction Agreements with the Commitments as well as
         the Trustee’s Final Reasoned Opinion thereof. The Commission considers that the
        Transaction Agreements will enable the Parties to transfer and Snipes to acquire
         the Divestment Business in a manner that is consistent with the Commitments.

(21)     First, the Commission considers that the Transaction Agreements correctly outline
         the scope of the Divestment Business to be transferred as agreed by the Parties and
        approved by the Commission in the Decision.

(22)    Moreover,  the Commission  finds  that  the  interim covenants and procedures
        contemplated  for the period  starting from the Decision  until Closing of the
        Divestment Business’ transaction, adequately address the need to maintain the
          viability of the Divestment Business so as Snipes acquires and further operates it as
        an active competitive force.

(23)     Further, the Commission deems  that the  Parties and Snipes have envisaged
         sufficient  protections  in order  to meet  the Closing date as included  in  the
       Commitments, and have so far, made good progress in meeting the necessary
         conditions to closing.

(24)    For the above reasons, the Commission agrees with the conclusions in the Trustee’s
         Final Reasoned Opinion that the Divestment Business will be transferred to Snipes
         in a manner consistent with the Commitments.

(25)    Moreover,  while  based on  the  Trustee’s  Final Reasoned Opinion  that  the
        Transaction Agreements are overall consistent with the spirit of the Commitments,
         the Commission notes that in case of conflict between the wording of the text of the
       Commitments and the Transaction Agreements executed by the Parties, Snipes, or
       any other party for the purposes of implementing the Commitments, the wording of
         the Commitments shall prevail, without prejudice to the fact that the interpretation
         of the Commitments shall take into account the solution that is more favourable to
         the Divestment Business. Deviations from the Commitments in the Transaction
       Agreements  are  only  valid  if  they  have  been  expressly  approved by  the
       Commission.

4.     CONCLUSION
(26)   On the basis of the above assessment, the Commission approves Snipes as a
         suitable Purchaser.

(27)   On the basis of the Transaction Agreements, the Commission concludes that the
        Divestment Business is being sold in a manner consistent with the Commitments.

(28)    This decision only constitutes approval of the proposed Purchaser identified herein
       and  of  the  Transaction  Agreements.  This  decision  does  not  constitute  a
        confirmation that JD Sports and Courir have complied with their Commitments.

(29)    This decision is based on the Commitments attached to the Commission Decision
         of 22 October 2024 as Annex 2.
