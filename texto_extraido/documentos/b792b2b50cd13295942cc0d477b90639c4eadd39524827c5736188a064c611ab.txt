Dear Sir or Madam,


1.    FACTS AND PROCEDURE
(1)    By decision of 4 April 2025 (the “Decision”) based on Article 6(1)(b) in
        connection  with  Article 6(2)  of  Council  Regulation  (EC) No  139/2004
         (the “Merger Regulation”), (1) the Commission declared the operation by which
        Safran S.A. (“Safran”, France, or “Notifying Party”) acquires sole control over
         part of the actuation business of Collins Aerospace (“Target”), a business unit of
     RTX Corporation (“RTX”, United States) compatible with the internal market
         subject to conditions and obligations set out in the final commitments submitted
       on 27 March 2025 and annexed to the Decision (the “Commitments”).

(2)      In particular, under the Commitments, Safran commits to divest to an independent
       and suitable purchaser, with proven expertise in aerospace actuation, its North
       American actuation business, composed of Safran’s electric trimmable horizontal

2.    ASSESSMENT OF THE PROPOSAL
(6)     According to paragraphs 20 and 21 of the Commitments, the Commission must
         verify that the Divestment Business is being sold in a manner consistent with the
       Commitments and that the purchaser fulfils the purchaser criteria. In particular,
         in its assessment of the Proposed Purchaser, the Commission must verify that:
          (a)    Woodward is independent and unconnected to Safran, considering the
                  situation following the divestiture;
         (b)    Woodward has the financial resources to maintain and develop the
               Divestment Business as a  viable and  active competitive  force  in
                competition with Safran and other competitors. In  particular,  that
             Woodward  is not excessively leveraged, has  sufficient equity and
                    profitability, has a turnover significantly larger than the turnover of the
               Divestment Business, and has sufficient means to finance the acquisition
                 of the Divestment Business and to execute its business plan, including
                 the capability to invest in the Divestment Business;
          (c)    Woodward has proven expertise in aerospace actuation and the ability
               and incentive to maintain and develop the Divestment Business as
                a viable and active competitive force in competition with Safran and
                 other competitors;
         (d)      the acquisition of the Divestment Business by Woodward must neither
               be likely to create prima facie competition concerns nor give rise to a risk
                   that the implementation of the Commitments will be delayed.
