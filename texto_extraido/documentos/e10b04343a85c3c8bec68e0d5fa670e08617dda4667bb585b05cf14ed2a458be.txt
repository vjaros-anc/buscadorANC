M.11577 - EIFFAGE / EQOS
                         SECTION 1.2

                           Description of the concentration

The proposed concentration consists in the acquisition by Eiffage Énergie Systèmes –
Participations SAS ("EES"), a private limited company incorporated under the laws of France,
of 100% of EQOS Energie Deutschland GmbH ("EQOS" or the "Target"). As a result, EES
will acquire sole control over the Target within the meaning of Article 3(1)(b) of the Merger
Regulation.

EES is a French construction and concessions group operating mainly across the EU in energy
solutions including the design, building, operation and maintenance systems and facilities in
electrical, industrial and energy engineering and HVAC. EES is a wholly owned subsidiary of
Eiffage SA, a French multi-national conglomerate, also headquartered in France, offering
various solutions across four different business lines: (i) construction, (ii) infrastructure, (iii)
concessions and (iv) energy systems.

EQOS is a German-based multinational group specializing in the construction, maintenance
and optimization of infrastructure solutions in the areas of railway technology, overhead line
construction, energy, communications,  industrial technology and engineering. EQOS  is
currently wholly owned by funds advised and managed by Triton, which is an international
investment firm, investing primarily in German-speaking countries, the Nordic countries, the
Benelux region, France, Italy, Spain and the United Kingdom, and which focuses on medium-
sized businesses operating in the Industrial, Business Services and Consumer/Health sectors.
