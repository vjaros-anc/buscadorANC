M.12161 – BH / CHART

                         SECTION 1.2

                           Description of the concentration

The concentration concerns the following undertakings:

     -  Baker Hughes Company (“BH”, United States of America), and

     -   Chart Industries, Inc. (“Chart”, United States of America).

BH will acquire, within the meaning of Article 3(1), point (b) of the Merger Regulation, sole

control of the whole of Chart.

The concentration will be accomplished by means of an acquisition of shares.

The business activities of the undertakings concerned are:

     -  BH is an energy technology company chain operating in more than 120 countries, with

       a diversified portfolio of technologies and services that span the energy and industrial

       value chains.

     -   Chart designs, engineers, and manufactures process technologies and equipment for

       gas and  liquid molecule  handling  for LNG,  hydrogen,  biogas,  industrial  gas,

        industrials and CO2 capture applications.  It operates 64 manufacturing  facilities

      worldwide and provides services globally.
