1.     FACTS AND PROCEDURE

(1)    By decision of 26 June 2024  (the “Decision”) based on  Article  6(1)(b)  in
        conjunction  with  Article  6(2)  of  Council  Regulation No  139/20041,  the
       Commission declared the acquisition of sole control by Cooper Consumer Health
        S.A.S. (“Cooper”, France)2 over the European over-the-counter (“OTC”) business
         of  Viatris  Inc.  (the “Target”, France and  Italy)  (together with Cooper,  the
        “Parties”) compatible with the internal market, subject to the conditions and
         obligations set out in the final Otowaxol commitments submitted on 24 June 2024
       and annexed to the Decision (the “Commitments”).3

        purchaser or purchasers:
        (a)  The  rights,  title and  interests in Bebegel including the right to develop,

        (b)  The rights,  title and interests in Otowaxol including the right to develop,
            manufacture and use Otowaxol, with a view to its sale and marketing in any
           form in Germany and, at the purchaser's option, in Ireland (the “Otowaxol
           Divestment Business”).

(3)     This decision concerns the approval of the proposed purchaser of the Otowaxol
        Divestment Business.

(4)    By the reasoned proposal dated 4 October 2024 (the “Reasoned Proposal”),
       Cooper   proposed   InfectoPharm   Arzneimittel   und   Consilium  GmbH
        (“InfectoPharm”, Germany) for approval by the Commission as purchaser of the
       Otowaxol Divestment Business. Cooper also submitted a copy of the proposed
         asset purchase agreement  to  the Commission which was  entered  into with
        InfectoPharm on 27 September 2024 (the “Otowaxol APA”).

(5)     The  Otowaxol APA  was  amended  on  21  November  2024  (the “APA
       Amendment”). The APA Amendment was submitted to the Commission on
       22 November 2024. The Otowaxol APA as amended by the APA Amendment
         together with its schedules including a transitional services agreement between
      Meda Pharma S.p.A (“Meda”), a subsidiary of Cooper, and InfectoPham relating
         to the recertification process of Otowaxol4 (the “TSA”) and Form of Right to Use
         Viatris Names are referred to as the “Transaction Documents”.

(6)     The Otowaxol APA sets out the terms of the sale and transfer of the Otowaxol
        Divestment Business to InfectoPharm. The Transaction Documents are conditional
       on the Commission approving InfectoPharm as the purchaser of the Otowaxol
        Divestment Business.

(7)     Under the Otowaxol APA, Cooper shall use its best efforts to obtain the necessary
        consent to assign the relevant parts of the manufacturing contract related to
       Otowaxol  to  InfectoPharm.  The  Otowaxol APA  foresees  a  back-to-back
        arrangement between Cooper and InfectoPharm for a duration of up to three years
         in case an assignment cannot be obtained on substantially the same terms and
         conditions.

(8)    On 22 November 2024, Monitoring Trustee Partners B.V. (“MTP”), acting as the
        monitoring trustee (the “Trustee”), submitted its assessment of InfectoPharm’s
          suitability as a purchaser (the “Reasoned Opinion”). In particular, the Trustee
         indicated that  it considers InfectoPharma to fulfil the criteria of the purchaser
        requirements set out in paragraph 16 of the Commitments. In this assessment, the
        Trustee also indicated  that, on the basis of the Transaction Documents, the
       Otowaxol Divestment Business would be sold in a manner consistent with the
       Commitments.

2.     ASSESSMENT OF THE PROPOSAL

(9)     According to paragraphs 16 and 17 of the Commitments, the purchaser must fulfil
         certain criteria as provided below (the “Purchaser Criteria”) and the divestment
        businesses are to be sold in a manner consistent with the Commitments, including
          their objective to bring about a lasting structural change in the market.

2.1.    Assessment of the Purchaser Criteria

(10)   As set out in paragraph 16 of the Commitments, the purchaser of the Otowaxol
        Divestment Business must fulfil the following criteria:
        (a)  The Purchaser(s) shall be independent of and unconnected to the Notifying
            Party and their Affiliated Undertakings (this being assessed having regard to
             the situation following the divestiture);
        (b)  The Purchaser(s) shall have the financial resources, proven expertise and
             incentive to maintain and develop the relevant Divestment Business as a viable
           and  active  competitive  force  in  competition  with  Cooper  and  other
             competitors;
        (c)  The acquisition of the relevant Divestment Business by the Purchaser(s) must
             neither be  likely  to  create,  in  light  of the information available  to the
           Commission, prima facie competition concerns nor give rise to a risk that the
            implementation  of  the Commitments  will be  delayed.  In  particular,  the
            Purchaser(s) must reasonably be expected to obtain all necessary approvals
           from the relevant regulatory authorities for the acquisition of the Divestment
             Businesses; and
        (d)  (…) the Purchaser of the Otowaxol Divestment Business must have a sales and
            marketing presence and access to an appropriate distribution network in
          Germany  in  the  relevant  sales  channels  (including  wholesalers  and
            pharmacies).

2.1.1.   Description of the proposed purchaser

(11)    InfectoPharm  is a German family-owned pharmaceutical company focused on
        medicines and medical devices. Initially the company was focused on paediatric
         infectiology with a niche portfolio of unique oral liquid formulations of antibiotics
         for  children.  It has since expanded  its  portfolio  to encompass several other
         paediatric products, as well as dermatology, otolaryngology, pneumology and
         allergology.

(12)    Currently InfectoPharm’s  portfolio encompasses approximately 130  products.
        Besides  medicines,  the  company  also  distributes  medical  devices,  food
        supplements, and cosmetic products. The products marketed by InfectoPharm
         originate either from  its own R&D  activities or from cooperation with other
        pharmaceutical companies.

(13)    InfectoPharm, with around 400 employees, has expanded internationally over that
          last  decade,  currently  delivering  to  approximately  40  countries  through
         subsidiaries, distribution partners, and tender businesses.

2.1.2.   Independence from Cooper

(14)    Pursuant to paragraph 16(a) of the Commitments, a suitable purchaser must be
        independent of and unconnected to Cooper and its affiliated undertakings.

(15)    Based  on  the  Trustee’s  review  of  information  provided  by  Cooper  and
        InfectoPharm, there are no common board directors, cross-shareholdings or other
          institutional ties between InfectoPharm and Cooper and its affiliated undertakings
         (including CVC  portfolio companies), nor any material commercial or other
         relationships.

(16)   One licence and supply agreement is in place between InfectoPharm and Genetic
        S.p.A.  (“Genetic”),  a  contract development and  manufacturing  organisation
      (“CDMO”) controlled by CVC. The licence and supply agreement relates to the
        product […]. The total input costs paid by InfectoPharm to Genetic for the supply
         of […]. Moreover, the annual sales of […] are expected to account  for an
         insignificant share of InfectoPharm’s overall turnover in the upcoming years.

(17)    InfectoPharm and Cooper have confirmed that there are no additional collaboration
        agreements, joint-ventures, licensing agreements and acquisition or divestiture
          activities between InfectoPharm and any CVC portfolio companies (including
        Cooper).

(18)    Pursuant to the TSA, Cooper (through  its subsidiary Meda) will offer certain
         transitional services to InfectoPharm as part of the transfer of the Otowaxol
        Divestment Business. Otowaxol has been reclassified as a Class IIa medical device
        under the Medical Device Regulation5 (“MDR”), and therefore needs  to be
         recertified by a designated  “notified body”.6 Meda has  this approval  for a
         transitional period potentially until December 31, 2028. A change in the registered
         legal manufacturer during the transitional period would likely terminate  it and
        prolong the recertification procedure. Therefore, Otowaxol’s recertification as a
        Class IIa device needs to be completed before InfectoPharm can assume legal
        manufacturer  status.  This  recertification  process,  including  submission  of  a
         technical file and inspections, typically takes around 18 months. Meda intends to
        submit necessary documentation by early 2025, aiming for recertification by mid-
        2026, and will in the meantime support InfectoPharm through the TSA, remaining
         the legal manufacturer and covering related costs during this period. Until the
        change of legal manufacturer, InfectoPharm will act as Otowaxol's distributor.

(19)    The links arising from the TSA are temporary and are to be implemented to ensure
         the continuing viability of the Otowaxol Divestment Business. Furthermore, this
        arrangement will not enable Cooper or its affiliated undertakings to affect, directly
         or indirectly, InfectoPharm’s strategic behaviour.

(20)    Based on its review, the Trustee has concluded that there are no structural and only
         certain  limited  commercial  relationships  between Cooper  and  its  affiliated
        undertakings on the one side, and InfectoPharm, on the other side. The Trustee

(21)    Based on  the  information above and  the  Trustee’s Reasoned  Opinion,  the
       Commission concludes that InfectoPharm fulfils the purchaser criterion of being

2.1.3.   Financial resources, proven expertise and incentive to maintain and develop the
        Divested Business as a viable and active competitor

(22)    Pursuant to paragraph 16(b) of the Commitments, the purchaser shall have the
         financial resources, proven expertise and incentive to maintain and develop the
       Otowaxol Divestment Business  as a  viable and  active competitive  force  in
        competition with Cooper and other competitors.

2.1.3.1. Financial resources

(23)    In 2023, InfectoPharm’s total consolidated sales reached […], an […] compared to
         the previous year.  Its EBITDA for 2023 amounted to […], with an EBITDA
        margin of […]. Gross profit represented approximately […] of total sales.

(24)    InfectoPharm’s balance sheet reflects a consolidated equity of […] and a negative
         net financial debt position of […]. InfectoPharm has shown […] across key
         financial metrics, including sales, EBITDA, margin, equity, and net financial debt
       from FY 2021 through FY 2023.

(25)    The acquisition of the Otowaxol Divestment Business will be funded […]. As of
        mid-October 2024, InfectoPharm held immediately available funds of […]. These
        resources  are  sufficient  to  support  the  acquisition and any working  capital
        requirements of the Otowaxol Divestment Business.

(26)    The  Trustee deems InfectoPharm  to have  sufficient  financial  resources and

        going forward.

(27)    Based on the information above and the Trustee’s Reasoned Opinion on the
         financial situation of InfectoPharm, the Commission concludes that InfectoPharm
          fulfils the criterion of having the financial resources to maintain and develop the
       Otowaxol Divestment Business as a viable and active competitive force.

2.1.3.2. Proven experience

(28)    InfectoPharm possesses over 35 years of experience in the German pharmaceutical
        market, managing relationships with otorhinolaryngology, or ear, nose and throat
       (“ENT”) specialists and pharmacies in Germany. The company has also been
         active in the hospital sector.

(29)    InfectoPharm  is  a  marketing  authorisation  holder  and  distributor  for  over
       130 products (medicines, medical devices, food supplements and cosmetics) and
        possesses an established distribution network. InfectoPharm is able to supply all
       German pharmacies via  its network of third party pharmaceutical wholesalers,
       which is the main distribution channel for selling Otowaxol products.

(30)    Other  distribution  channels  for  Otowaxol  include  e-commerce  channels
           (i.e. e-pharmacies and Amazon). The Trustee understands that Otowaxol would
        continue to be available via these e-commerce channels, and that no relevant
        impact for the customers and, hence, for the viability of the Divestment Business,
           is to be expected.

(31)    The Trustee notes that InfectoPharm possesses regulatory  capabilities, having
         material knowledge of the underlying timelines, and has long term experience and
       know-how  in the  field of medical devices including also MDR  certification
         processes.7

(32)    In addition, InfectoPharm has prior experience in corporate carve outs and handling
         of transitional services. InfectoPharm recently acquired and integrated several OTC
        products in Germany including Eubiol (a OTC drug for the treatment of diarrhoea)
       and Verrucid (an OTC product for the treatment of warts, corns and calluses). From
       2019 to 2024, InfectoPharm has completed 21 transactions with 25 products in
          total,  including  both  acquisitions  and  licensing  deals.  In  aggregate,  these
         transactions included more than 100 marketing authorisations in different countries
       and more than 100 SKUs.

(33)    The Trustee is satisfied that InfectoPharm has the required expertise as required by
         the Purchaser Criteria.

(34)    Based on  the  information above and  the  Trustee’s Reasoned  Opinion,  the
       Commission considers that InfectoPharm has the proven experience to maintain
       and develop the Otowaxol Divestment Business as a viable and active competitive
         force.

2.1.3.3. Incentive

(35)    InfectoPharm has explained that  it perceives Otowaxol as a strong brand and
        considers the Otowaxol Divestment Business a good match with the company’s
        growth strategy as target groups of ENT specialists and pharmacies are already the
        focus target groups of InfectoPharm’s operations, particularly in Germany, where it
        has a strong presence in the ENT segment.

(36)    The Reasoned Opinion concludes that InfectoPharm has the incentive and objective
         to  maintain and  develop  the  Divestment  Business  as  a  viable and  active
        competitive force. This conclusion  is supported by the Otowaxol Divestment
        Business’s ability to generate solid cash flows on a standalone basis, as illustrated
         in its business plan. Otowaxol also complements InfectoPharm’s existing Rx and
     OTC portfolio, strengthening its ENT product range and aligning with its strategic
         goals.

(37)    Based on  the  information above and  the  Trustee’s Reasoned  Opinion,  the
       Commission considers  that InfectoPharm has the incentives  to maintain and
        develop the Otowaxol Divestment Business as a viable and active competitive

2.1.4.   Absence of prima facie competition problems or any risk of implementation delays

(38)    Pursuant to paragraph 16(c) of the Commitments, the acquisition of the Otowaxol
        Divestment Business by the purchaser must neither be likely to create prima facie
        competition concerns nor give  rise to a  risk  that the implementation of the
       Commitments will be delayed.

(39)    With  regard  the  competitive  interactions between  the Otowaxol Divestment
        Business and InfectoPharm:
        (a)   InfectoPharm’s product portfolio does not include any products for earwax
            removal, and  it  is therefore not active in the manufacture or supply of
            pharmaceutical products in the same ATC4 class or with the same molecule as
           Otowaxol.
        (b)   InfectoPharm’s current portfolio includes, amongst other products, two OTC
             ear drops products (OtoDolor and OtoAkut), both of which are included in the
          same broader ATC3 category S2D (Other Ear Preparations). However, these
            products are not comparable to Otowaxol as neither are used to remove excess
           earwax and earwax build-up but are instead intended for the treatment of otitis
             externa (inflammation of the external ear canal) or otitis otalgia (ear pain). In
           any case, InfectoPharm’s increment in this broader ATC3 category S2D would
           be limited, below 3%, in Germany.
        (c)   InfectoPharm has confirmed that its acquisition of the Otowaxol Divestment
            Business will not result in any vertically affected markets.

(40)    Based on information provided by InfectoPharm to the Trustee, InfectoPharm’s
         acquisition of the Otowaxol Divestment Business does not require any merger
         control or other regulatory filings.

(41)    The recertification process described above in paragraph (18) will not impact the
        implementation of the divestment.

(42)    Based on the information available, the Trustee considers that the approval of
        InfectoPharm as the Purchaser of the Otowaxol Divestment Business would not
         create any prima facie competition concerns, nor would it give rise to a risk that the
        implementation of the Commitments will be delayed.

(43)    Based on  the  information above and  the  Trustee’s Reasoned  Opinion,  the
       Commission considers that InfectoPharm’s acquisition of the Otowaxol Divestment
        Business would not create any prima facie competition concerns, nor would it give
          rise to a risk that the implementation of the Commitments will be delayed.

2.1.5.   Sales and market presence, and access to an appropriate distribution network, in
       Germany

(44)    Pursuant to paragraph 16(d) of the Commitments, a suitable purchaser must have a
         sales and marketing presence and access to an appropriate distribution network in
       Germany in the relevant sales channels (including wholesalers and pharmacies).

(45)   As  discussed  in  the  Section  2.1.3.2  above,  InfectoPharm  is  a  marketing
         authorisation holder and distributor of over 130 products and is able to supply all
       German pharmacies via its network of third party pharmaceutical wholesalers, the

2.1.6.   Conclusion

(48)    In view of the above, the Commission considers that InfectoPharm meets the
        purchaser criteria as laid down in paragraph 16 of the Commitments.

2.2.    Transaction Documents

(49)    Paragraph 17 of the Commitments states: “Cooper must be able to demonstrate to
         the Commission that the purchaser(s) fulfils the Purchaser Criteria and that the
        Divestment Businesses is being sold in a manner consistent with the Commission's
        Decision and the Commitments”.

(50)   As described above in paragraph (5), the Transaction Documents consist of the
       Otowaxol APA as amended by the APA Amendment and its schedules, which
        include the TSA and Form of Right to Use Viatris Names. The TSA and Form of
        Right to Use Viatris Names are in their agreed form and will be executed at the
        time of closing of the sale of the Otowaxol Divestment Business.

(51)    The Commission has been provided with copies of the Transaction Documents.
       The Commission has reviewed the Transaction Documents and is satisfied that
         these enable the Otowaxol Divestment Business to be sold in accordance with the
       Commitments.

(52)    The Trustee has also confirmed the terms of the Transaction Documents to
         materially reflect the Parties’ obligations set out in the Commitments.

(53)    With regard to the TSA which is not reflected in the Commitments, the Trustee
        notes that Cooper shall use its best efforts to support the recertification process of
       Otowaxol and will pay for all costs that will be incurred in this process.

(54)    Based on  the  information above and  the  Trustee’s Reasoned  Opinion,  the
       Commission concludes that the Transaction Documents are consistent with the
       Commitments, and that the Otowaxol Divestment Business is being transferred in a
       manner consistent with the Commitments.
