(1)    By decision of 30 October 2023 (the ‘Decision’) based on Article 6(1)(b) in

        business of Thales SA (‘Thales GTS’) (the ‘Concentration’) compatible with the
         internal market, subject to conditions and obligations (the ‘Commitments’).
        Hitachi Rail and Thales GTS are together referred to as the ‘Parties’.

(2)     The Commitments provide that Hitachi Rail commits to divest its rail signalling
        business in France and Germany, complemented by the divestiture of Hitachi

2.     ASSESSMENT OF THE PURCHASER PROPOSAL

2.1.    Legal framework

(5)     According to paragraph 20 of the Commitments, the Commission has to verify
         that the purchaser fulfils the purchaser criteria and that the Divestment Business
           is being sold in a manner consistent with the Commitments.

(6)     According to paragraph 19 of the Commitments, in  its assessment of the
        proposed purchaser, the Commission should ensure in particular that:
          (a)  The purchaser shall be independent of and unconnected to Hitachi Rail
            and its affiliated undertakings (this being assessed having regard to the
               situation following the divestiture);
         (b)  The purchaser shall have the financial resources and strength, proven
               expertise and experience in the rail infrastructure industry, international
              presence, incentive and objective to maintain and develop the Divestment
             Business as a viable and active competitive force in competition with the
               Parties and other competitors;
          (c)  The acquisition of the Divestment Business by the purchaser must neither
             be likely to create, in light of the information available to the Commission,
            prima  facie  competition  concerns  nor  give  rise  to  a  risk  that  the
             implementation of the Commitments will be delayed. In particular, the
              purchaser must reasonably be expected to obtain all necessary approvals
            from  the  relevant  regulatory  authorities  for  the  acquisition  of  the
             Divestment Business.

(7)     The Concentration shall not be implemented before Hitachi Rail has entered into
        a final binding sale and purchase agreement for the sale of the Divestment
        Business and the Commission has approved the purchaser and the terms of sale.
        For the purchaser approval, paragraph 20 of the Commitments specifies that the
       Commission  shall be  satisfied  that SNCF and Deutsche Bahn would not
        withhold  their consent  to  the  transfer  of  their backlog  contracts3  to  the
         purchaser.

2.3.3.   Absence of prima facie competition problems or risk of delayed implementation

(40)    According to paragraph 19(c) of the Commitments, the acquisition of the
        Divestment Business by the purchaser must neither be likely to create prima
         facie competition concerns nor give rise to a risk that the implementation of the
       Commitments will be delayed.

(41)    In light of the data submitted by Mer Mec and Hitachi Rail, the acquisition by
      Mer Mec of the Divestment Business is unlikely to raise prima facie competition
        concerns. This  is because such an acquisition would give  rise  to limited
         horizontal overlaps at EEA level only,40 with regard to41 (i) ETCS ATP wayside
         resignalling projects in the EEA, (ii) ETCS ATP wayside overlay projects in the
     EEA and (iii) ETCS OBU projects in the EEA. However, none of these markets
           is affected as the combined market shares of the new entity would remain below
        [0-5]% under any plausible market definition.42

(42)    The acquisition of the Divestment Business by Mer Mec is also unlikely to face
         additional obstacles which could delay its implementation. In particular, the
         acquisition does not trigger merger control filings and only triggers two foreign
         direct investments (‘FDI’) filings in [EU Member State] and [third country].
      Mer Mec has already formally notified the transaction, in [EU Member State],
       on [confidential information on date of notification], and in [third country], on
         [confidential information on date of notification]. According to Mer Mec, the
         acquisition of the Divestment Business should be cleared by [EU Member State]
       and [third country] authorities by early June 2024.43

(43)    This is consistent with the reasoned opinion of the Trustee, who does not expect
       any obstacle in obtaining the relevant clearances from the relevant [EU Member
         State] and [third country] authorities within the standard review period.44

(44)    In view of the foregoing, the Commission concludes that the acquisition of the
        Divestment Business by Mer Mec does not create prima facie competition
        concerns nor does  it give  rise  to a  risk  that  the implementation of  the
       Commitments will be delayed.

2.3.4.  Main customers’ consents to the transfer of their contracts to Mer Mec

(45)    According to paragraph 20 of the Commitments, in order to approve the
         purchaser, the Commission shall be satisfied that the consents from SNCF and
        Deutsche Bahn in relation to the transfer of their relevant backlog contracts (as
        defined in the schedule of the Commitments) to the purchaser would not be
        withheld.

(46)   On 25 March 2024, SNCF Réseau, SNCF Voyageurs and Deutsche Bahn signed
         three consent letters by which they expressly confirmed that they do not intend
         to withhold their consents for the transfer of their ongoing contracts with Hitachi
         Rail, as a result of the acquisition of the Divestment Business by Mer Mec.

(47)   As Deutsche Bahn explained: ‘Mermec does not seem new to the signalling
         business, and they seem to have a good base to build up a future business.
      Mermec would  still need to go through certification processes to have their
        products  certified by  the Company  [Deutsche  Bahn]. However, Mermec
        probably fulfils requirements in other countries that might be similar to those in
         application in Germany’. 45

(48)    In view of the foregoing, the Commission is satisfied that SNCF Réseau, SNCF
       Voyageurs and Deutsche Bahn will not withhold their consent to the transfer of
          their contracts to Mer Mec.

(53)     First, the Proposed Agreements stipulate that only [0-5] ‘Italy-based suitably
         qualified engineers from Hitachi Rail’ will be seconded to the Divestment
        Business, contrary to the minimum number of [5-10] initially foreseen in the
        Commitments.49 The purpose of this secondment program was to achieve the
        homologation of the signalling platforms being divested, to ensure the effective
         transfer of the divested platforms and to further strengthen the competitiveness
         of the Divestment Business.

(54)    Based on the Trustee’s assessment, the Commission considers that the range of
         technological skills required by the Divestment Business is potentially wide and
      may therefore require input from a large number of specialists, though only
        a limited number of hours from each. A secondment of a large number of
        engineers would therefore not be the optimal solution to address the Divestment
        Business’ needs. In this context, Mer Mec, the Hold-Separate Manager and
        Hitachi Rail commonly agreed that it would be in the interest of Mer Mec and
         the Divestment Business to ensure that the new entity has access to a limited
       number of hours of a broad range of specialized people. As a  result, the
        Divestment Business has carefully selected [0-5] individuals that not only have
         the right skills but also know the Divestment Business, and can serve as contact
         points to personnel retained by Hitachi Rail which may hold required expertise.
        In any event, Mer Mec will remain able to request further seconded employees
        according to its business needs (up to [5-10]).50

(55)    This  is consistent with the view expressed by the Trustee in his reasoned
         opinion,  according  to which  this  solution  will  not  adversely impact  the
         effectiveness of the Commitments.51
