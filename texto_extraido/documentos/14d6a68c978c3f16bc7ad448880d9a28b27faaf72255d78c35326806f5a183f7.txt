M.11043 - NOVOZYMES / CHR HANSEN HOLDING

                         SECTION 1.2

                           Description of the concentration

   (1)    On 20 October 2023, the Commission received a notification of a proposed
          combination of Novozymes A/S ("Novozymes") and Chr. Hansen Holding A/S
           ("Chr.  Hansen  Holding")  with  Novozymes  as  the  surviving  entity  (the
           "Transaction").
   (2)     Novozymes is a publicly listed company incorporated under the laws of Denmark.
         Novozymes  is  solely  controlled by Novo Holdings A/S, a  limited  liability
         company incorporated under the laws of Denmark.
   (3)     Chr. Hansen Holding is a publicly listed company incorporated under the laws of
         Denmark. Chr. Hansen Holding owns 100 % of the shares in Chr. Hansen A/S and
           other subsidiaries (Chr. Hansen Holding and subsidiaries directly or indirectly
           controlled by Chr. Hansen Holding, including Chr. Hansen A/S, are hereinafter
            referred to as the "Chr. Hansen Group").
   (4)     The Transaction entails that Novozymes and Chr. Hansen Holding will combine
           within the meaning of Article 3(1)(a) of the Merger Regulation with Novozymes as
           the  surviving  entity and  Chr. Hansen  Holding’s  subsidiaries  consequently
         becoming subsidiaries of Novozymes.
   (5)     The parties to the Transaction are both active in the worldwide biotech industry, but
           the respective main business areas of the parties and the markets in which they
           operate are to a large extent different and complementary.
   (6)     Novozymes is a global bioscience company and is predominantly a developer,
          manufacturer and supplier of industrial enzymes to be used within household care,
           bioenergy, grain & tech processing, agriculture and animal health & nutrition, and
          food & beverage and, to a lesser extent, a manufacturer of probiotics for human
         and animal health and biologicals for plant health.
   (7)     Chr. Hansen Group is a global differentiated bioscience company that develops
           natural  ingredients  solutions  for  the  food,  nutritional,  pharmaceutical  and
            agricultural industries for a variety of foods, beverages, dietary supplements,
          animal  feed and  crop  protection.  Chr. Hansen Group  is  predominantly a
          manufacturer and supplier of cultures for food & beverage, but, to a lesser extent,
           also manufactures probiotics for human and animal health, biologicals for plant
           health and enzymes.
