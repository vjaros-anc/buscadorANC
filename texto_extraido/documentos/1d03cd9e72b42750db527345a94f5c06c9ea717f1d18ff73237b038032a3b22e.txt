1.     FACTS AND PROCEDURE

(1)    By decision of 26 June 2024  (the “Decision”) based on  Article 6(1)(b)  in

        S.A.S. (“Cooper”, France)2 over the European over-the-counter (“OTC”) business
         of  Viatris  Inc.  (the “Target”, France and  Italy)  (together with Cooper, the
        “Parties”) compatible with the internal market, subject to the conditions and
         obligations set out in the final commitments submitted on 24 June 2024 and
       annexed to the Decision (the “Commitments”).3

(2)      In particular, under the Commitments, the Parties undertook to divest to a suitable
        purchaser or purchasers:
          (a)  The rights,  title and interests in Bebegel including the right to develop,
             manufacture and use Bebegel, with a view to its sale and marketing in any
            form in Portugal and, at the purchaser’s option, in France (the “Bebegel
            Divestment Business”); and
         (b)  The rights, title and interests in Otowaxol including the right to develop,
             manufacture and use Otowaxol, with a view to its sale and marketing in any
            form in Germany and, at the purchaser's option, in Ireland.

(3)     This decision concerns the approval of the proposed purchaser of the Bebegel
        Divestment Business.

(4)    By the reasoned proposal dated 8 October 2024, Cooper proposed Grupo J. Uriach,
         S.L.  (Spain)  (together  with  its  subsidiaries “Uriach”)  for  approval by  the
       Commission as purchaser of the Bebegel Divestment Business. The Bebegel
        Divestment Business is intended to be held by Uriach Lusa S.A. (“Uriach Lusa”,
         Portugal). Cooper also submitted a copy of the proposed asset purchase agreement
        entered into with Uriach Lusa on 1 October 2024 (the “Bebegel APA”).

(5)     The Bebegel APA was amended on 28 November 2024 (the “APA Amendment”).
       The APA Amendment was submitted to the Commission on 11 December 2024.
       The Bebegel APA as amended by the APA Amendment together with its schedules
        including a  transitional services agreement  for the  transfer of the marketing
         authorisation4 (the “TSA”) and Form of Right to Use Viatris Names are referred to
         as the “Transaction Documents”.

(6)     The Bebegel APA sets out the terms of the sale and transfer of the Bebegel
        Divestment Business to Uriach. The Transaction Documents are conditional on the
       Commission approving Uriach  as  the purchaser  of  the Bebegel Divestment
        Business and merger control clearance in Portugal.

(7)     Under the Bebegel APA, Cooper shall assign or use its best efforts to assign the
         relevant parts of the contract manufacturing agreement related to Bebegel to Uriach
        Lusa. The Bebegel APA foresees a back-to-back arrangement between Cooper and
        Uriach Lusa for a duration of up to three years in case an assignment cannot be
        obtained on substantially the same terms and conditions.

(8)    On 13 December 2024, Monitoring Trustee Partners B.V., acting as the monitoring
         trustee (the “Trustee”), submitted  its assessment of Uriach’s  suitability as a
        purchaser (the “Reasoned Opinion”). In particular, the Trustee indicated that  it
        considers Uriach to fulfil the criteria of the purchaser requirements set out in
        paragraph 16 of the Commitments. In the Reasoned Opinion, the Trustee also
         indicated that, on the basis of the Transaction Documents, the Bebegel Divestment
        Business would be sold in a manner consistent with the Commitments.

2.     ASSESSMENT OF THE PROPOSAL

(9)     According to paragraphs 16 and 17 of the Commitments, the purchaser must fulfil
         certain criteria as provided below (the “Purchaser Criteria”) and the divestment
        businesses are to be sold in a manner consistent with the Commitments including
          their objective to bring about a lasting structural change in the market.

2.1.    Assessment of the Purchaser Criteria

(10)   As set out in paragraph 16 of the Commitments, the purchaser(s) of the Bebegel
        Divestment Business must fulfil the following criteria:
         (a)  The Purchaser(s) shall be independent of and unconnected to the Notifying
             Party and their Affiliated Undertakings (this being assessed having regard to
              the situation following the divestiture);
         (b)  The Purchaser(s) shall have the financial resources, proven expertise and
               incentive to maintain and develop the relevant Divestment Business as a
              viable and active competitive force in competition with Cooper and other
              competitors;
          (c)  The acquisition of the relevant Divestment Business by the Purchaser(s) must
               neither be  likely to create, in light of the information available to the
            Commission, prima facie competition concerns nor give rise to a risk that the
             implementation  of the Commitments  will be delayed. In  particular, the
              Purchaser(s) must reasonably be expected to obtain all necessary approvals
            from the relevant regulatory authorities for the acquisition of the Divestment
              Businesses; and
         (d)  The Purchaser of the Bebegel Divestment Business must have a sales and
             marketing presence and access to an appropriate distribution network in
             Portugal in the relevant sales channels (including wholesalers, hospitals and
             pharmacies) (…).

2.1.1.   Description of the proposed purchaser

(11)    Uriach Lusa is the Portuguese wholly-owned subsidiary of Grupo J. Uriach, S.L.,
       which is jointly controlled by Corporación  J. Uriach, S.A. (Spain) (owned by
         several  members  of  the  Uriach  family)  and  Provence  Investment  Sàrl
        (Luxembourg), a special purpose vehicle, which in turn is indirectly controlled by
         the asset manager Intermediate Capital Group plc (“ICG”, United Kingdom).

(12)    Uriach is a Spanish pharmaceutical group, which was founded in 1838. Uriach is
         currently focused exclusively on natural consumer healthcare and  its portfolio
         includes brands such as Aquilea, EMS, Fisiocrem, Halibut, Laborest, Pediakid and
        Sidroga. In addition to its presence in Spain, Uriach has subsidiaries in Austria,
        Belgium, Canada, Czech Republic, France, Germany, Italy, Portugal (i.e. Uriach
        Lusa), Romania, Slovakia and Switzerland. In addition, Uriach has a strategic
         partnership for marketing its brands in Greece and has distribution agreements,
         currently covering over 30 countries worldwide.

(13)    Uriach entered the Portuguese market in 2015 by establishing its own salesforce to
       promote its Aquilea and Fisiocrem brands. Uriach has since expanded its presence
         in Portugal through various acquisitions. Uriach Lusa is based in Lisbon and has
       […]. Uriach Lusa has 13 active brands in Portugal, comprising of 85 products.

2.1.2.   Independence from Cooper

(14)    Pursuant to paragraph 16(a) of the Commitments, a suitable purchaser must be
        independent of and unconnected to Cooper and its Affiliated Undertakings.5

(15)   As noted above, Uriach is jointly controlled by Corporación J. Uriach S.A. and
       ICG. Cooper is solely controlled by CVC.

(16)    There are no common shareholders between Cooper, on the one hand, and Uriach
       and its Affiliated Undertakings, on the other hand nor vice versa (i.e. between
        Uriach, on the one hand, and Cooper and its Affiliated Undertakings on the other).
        Also, amongst the 20 largest shareholders of ICG and CVC, only one common
        shareholder exists, namely AllianceBernstein L.P., which holds below 3% of the
          total outstanding shares in each of the respective companies.

(17)    Cooper has confirmed that neither CVC nor any of its Affiliated Undertakings is
         invested in Uriach. Cooper has also confirmed that it does not have a shareholding
         in ICG. Moreover, CVC is not aware of any shareholdings that  it or any of its
         Affiliated Undertakings may have in ICG, and notes that if there were to be such a
        shareholding it would be below 1.8% (i.e. the percentage that the smallest publicly
       known shareholder has in ICG). Furthermore, neither Cooper nor CVC has any
        investments in any ICG Affiliated Undertaking.6

(18)    Uriach, on the other hand, has confirmed that neither ICG nor any of its Affiliated
        Undertakings has any shareholding in Cooper. Uriach has also confirmed that it
        does not have any shareholding in CVC. Furthermore, ICG (including its Affiliated
        Undertakings) does not have any investment in any CVC Affiliated Undertaking.7
      ICG has however invested in a number of active CVC Funds. These investments
         are small minority Limited Partnership (“LP”) interests that do not give ICG any
         strategic control over the respective funds or the fund’s investments.8

(19)    The above has also been confirmed by the Trustee’s independent research, which
        did not reveal any other links between Uriach and its Affiliated Undertakings, on
         the one hand, and CVC and its Affiliated Undertakings, on the other hand.

(20)    Cooper and Uriach have confirmed that no directors of Uriach or ICG hold
         directorships in CVC or any of its Affiliated Undertakings and that no directors of
       Cooper or CVC hold directorships in ICG or any of its Affiliated Undertakings.

       Commission  concludes  that  Uriach  fulfils  the  purchaser  criterion  of  being

2.1.3.2. Proven expertise

(32)    Uriach has a long history and proven expertise in supplying medical OTC products
         in numerous countries including Portugal. As explained above, Uriach has been
         active in Portugal since 2015 and has gradually expanded its presence through
         several  acquisitions. Today Uriach  is  a marketing  authorisation  holder and
         distributor for a portfolio that consists of over 85 products, covering medicines,
        medical devices, food supplements and cosmetics.

(33)    Uriach Lusa has a salesforce of […] and is active across the main sales channels in
       which  the  Bebegel  Divestment  Business  is  active,  including  wholesalers,
        pharmacies and pharmacy groups. More specifically, the company performs direct
         sales to approximately […] of the pharmacies in Portugal with indirect sales
        covering almost the  entirety of the remaining pharmacies. Moreover, Uriach
        already has pre-existing relationships with the top customers of the Bebegel
        Divestment Business.

(34)   As regards paediatric care, Uriach has some prior experience in this area outside of
        Portugal including Pediakid (dietary supplements  specifically for children) in
        France and Laborest (nutraceutical products including food supplements against a
        range of issues, such as respiratory health, digestion, and bone health) in Italy. The
        Trustee considers that such expertise could benefit and facilitate the integration of
         the Bebegel Divestment Business. Uriach has also noted that it does not foresee

2.1.3.3. Incentive

(38)    Uriach has explained that its interest in the Bebegel Divestment Business is mainly
        driven by the solid reputation of the business and the brand’s positioning in the
        Portuguese market, and that  it is consistent with its expansion strategy. Uriach
        considers Bebegel’s high quality and its broad channel distribution to be aligned
        with Uriach’s vision and business model.

(39)   As explained in further detail below in paragraph (45), the Bebegel Divestment
        Business is complementary to Uriach’s existing product portfolio (both in Portugal
       and  in other markets), thereby allowing  it  to  enter a new market segment.
        According to Uriach, Bebegel would become the fourth biggest product (in terms
         of sales) in Uriach’s Portuguese portfolio and the incorporation of the Bebegel
        Divestment Business would enhance  its position in the Portuguese market and
         financial performance, as well as support its long-term growth both in Portugal and
       on a consolidated level. This is further supported by the solid cash flow generation
         of the Bebegel Divestment Business’ on a standalone basis. Going forward, under
           its ownership Uriach aims to increase the sales of Bebegel through its commercial
         capabilities in Portugal.

(40)    Furthermore, the integration of the Bebegel Divestment Business is expected to be
         relatively straightforward given that  it involves only one product, already well
         established in Portugal.

(41)    Based on discussions between the Trustee and Uriach, the Trustee is confident that
        Uriach is dedicated to incorporating the Bebegel Divestment Business into the
        broader Uriach organisation as soon as possible post-closing, and to maintain and
       grow the business going forward. The Trustee notes that Uriach is partially a
        family-owned company and that the individuals who have decided to acquire the
        Bebegel Divestment Business have done so […], which should further incentivise
        Uriach to make an economic success of the acquisition of the Bebegel Divestment
        Business.

(42)    The Trustee’s Reasoned Opinion concluded that Uriach would have the incentive
       and objective to maintain the Bebegel Divestment Business as required by the
        Purchaser Criteria.

(43)    Based on the above, the Commission considers that Uriach has the incentives to
        maintain and develop the Bebegel Divestment Business as a viable and active
        competitive force in competition with Cooper and other competitors.

2.1.4.   Absence of prima facie competition problems

(44)    Pursuant to paragraph 16(c) of the Commitments, the acquisition of the Bebegel
        Divestment Business by the purchaser must neither be likely to create prima facie
        competition concerns nor give  rise to a  risk  that the implementation of the
       Commitments will be delayed.

(45)    With regard the competitive interactions between the Bebegel Divestment Business
       and Uriach:
          (a)   Uriach  is  not  active  in  Portugal  in  the  manufacture  or  supply  of
              pharmaceutical products in the same ATC3 or ATC4 class as Bebegel, nor
             does its portfolio include any products used to treat constipation in infants.
         (b)   For  completeness,  Uriach’s  current  portfolio  includes  products  against
              constipation supplied in Portugal under the Aquilea Lax and Depuralina
              brands. However, as these products are food supplements or medical devices,
            and not medicines, these do not give rise to any overlaps in the relevant
         ATC3 or ATC4 classes (i.e. A6A – drugs for constipation or A6A4 – laxative
             enemas). Moreover, considering that these products are not intended to treat
              constipation in infants, these do not appear to be substitutable with Bebegel.
          (c)   Uriach has confirmed that the transaction does not give rise to any vertical
                links.

(46)    The purchase of the Bebegel Divestment Business by Uriach is subject to approval
       by  the  Portuguese  competition  authority. The  acquisition  of  the  Bebegel
        Divestment Business by Uriach was  cleared by  the Portuguese competition
         authority on 28 November 2024. No other governmental or regulatory approvals
         required to complete the transaction have been identified.

(47)    Based on the information available, the Trustee considers that the approval of
        Uriach as the purchaser of the Bebegel Divestment Business would not create any
        prima  facie competition concerns, nor would  it give  rise  to a  risk  that the
        implementation of the Commitments will be delayed.

(48)    Based on the above, the Commission considers that the acquisition of the Bebegel
        Divestment Business by Uriach satisfies the criteria set out in paragraph 16(c) of
         the Commitments.

(49)    This prima facie assessment is based on the information available for the purpose
         of this buyer approval and does not prejudge the competition assessment of the
         acquisition  of  the Bebegel Divestment Business by Uriach by a competent
        competition authority under applicable merger control rules.

         in Portugal

         sales and marketing presence and access to an appropriate distribution network in
        Portugal  in the relevant  sales channels (including wholesalers,  hospitals and
        pharmacies).

(51)   As discussed in Section 2.1.3.2 above, Uriach is currently marketing 85 products in
        Portugal and has an appropriate distribution network in Portugal in the main sales
        channels  (i.e.  wholesalers  and  pharmacies). While  Uriach  is  not  currently
         distributing any of its products through the hospital channel in Portugal, at group
         level it has experience in this segment, and it is foreseen that Bebegel’s ongoing
         hospital business will be overseen by a senior Uriach Key Account Manager, with
        dedicated support from Uriach’s Iberian medical department team.  It is further
        noted that the Bebegel Divestment Business’ sales through the hospital channel are
         limited (approx. […] of the total revenues). In light of the limited presence of the
        Bebegel Divestment Business in the hospital channel and Uriach’s  ability to
        leverage hospital experience outside of Portugal, the Trustee does not consider
        Uriach’s absence in this channel in Portugal to be problematic.

(52)     In  its Reasoned Opinion, the Trustee found that Uriach  fulfils the Purchaser
         Criteria as included in paragraph 16(d) of the Commitments.

(53)    Based on this information and the Trustee’s Reasoned Opinion, the Commission
        considers that Uriach has the required sales and marketing presence, and access to
        an appropriate distribution network in Portugal in the relevant sales channels.

2.1.6.   Conclusion

(54)     In view of the above, the Commission concludes that Uriach meets the Purchaser
         Criteria as laid down in paragraph 16 of the Commitments.

2.2.    Transaction Documents

(55)    Paragraph 17 of the Commitments states: “Cooper must be able to demonstrate to

        Decision and the Commitments”.

(56)   As indicated in paragraph 5 above, the Transaction Documents consist of the
        Bebegel APA as amended by the APA Amendment and  its schedules, which
        include the TSA and Form of Right to Use Viatris Names. The TSA and Form of

(57)    The Commission has been provided with copies of the Transaction Documents.
       The Commission has reviewed the Transaction Documents and is satisfied that
         these enable the Bebegel Divestment Business to be sold in accordance with the
       Commitments.

(58)    The Trustee has also confirmed the terms of the Transaction Documents to be
         materially reflecting in Parties’ obligations set out in the Commitments.

(59)    Based on  the  information above and  the  Trustee’s Reasoned  Opinion,  the
       Commission concludes that the Transaction Documents are consistent with the
       Commitments and that the Bebegel Divestment Business is being transferred in a
       manner consistent with the Commitments.
