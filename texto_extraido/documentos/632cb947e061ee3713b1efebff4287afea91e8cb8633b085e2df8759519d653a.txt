M.11578 - BOEING / SPIRIT

                         SECTION 1.2

                           Description of the concentration


On August 26, 2025, the Commission received notification of a proposed acquisition of all
shares  in, and  thus  sole  control  of,  Spirit AeroSystems Holdings,  Inc.  (“Spirit”) by
The Boeing Company (“Boeing”) (the “Transaction”) (together the “Parties”). Boeing will
acquire (through Sphere Acquisition Corp., a newly created subsidiary directly and wholly
owned by Boeing) 100% of the issued share capital of Spirit at the closing date. Both Spirit
and Boeing are incorporated in Delaware, USA. The Transaction is notified under Article
3(1)(b) of the EUMR.

The activities of the Parties are as follows:

   •  Boeing, a US company headquartered in Arlington, Virginia, designs, manufactures,
      and sells commercial airplanes, defense, space and security systems, and provides
        related services. Boeing sells its products and provides related services on a global
        basis. Boeing has no controlling shareholder.

   •   Spirit, a US company headquartered in Wichita, Kansas, manufactures aerostructures,
       serving commercial aircraft, military aircraft and business/regional jets. Spirit also
       provides aftermarket services for commercial and military platforms. Spirit sells its
       products and provides related services on a global basis. Spirit has no controlling
       shareholder.

By reintegrating Spirit, Boeing can fully align its commercial production systems, including
its safety and quality management systems, and its workforce to the same priorities, incentives
and outcomes centered on safety and quality. Thus, this acquisition will directly enable efforts
to improve manufacturing quality, safety and production stability.

Boeing believes that this deal is in the best interest of the flying public, customers, and the
workers of Spirit and Boeing.
