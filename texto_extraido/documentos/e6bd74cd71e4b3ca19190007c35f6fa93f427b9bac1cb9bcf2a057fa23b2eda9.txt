M.10341 - PRINCE / FERRO

                          SECTION 1.2

                           Description of the concentration


On 11 May 2021, ASP Prince Holdings,  Inc. (“Prince”) announced that  it would acquire
Ferro Corporation (“Ferro”) (the “Proposed Transaction”). Prince  is a portfolio company
under  the  control of  affiliated  funds managed by American  Securities LLC (“American
Securities”).

In addition, as a part of the Proposed Transaction, Chromaflo Technologies (“Chromaflo”),
another  portfolio company which  is  controlled by  affiliated  funds managed by American
Securities,  will be combined with the Prince group of companies.


Prince   is  a US  corporation  headquartered  in  Houston,  Texas, USA.   It   is  a  global
manufacturer  of  mineral-based  chemicals,  minerals  and  industrial  additives and  products.
Prince was  established  in 1858 when Robert  Prince  founded  the  Prince  Manufacturing
Company as a supplier of colourants and additives  to the North American brick industry.

Ferro  is a US public company listed on the New York Stock Exchange, and headquartered
in  Mayfield  Heights, Ohio, USA. Ferro has approximately 6,000 employees  globally and
operates 48  facilities worldwide. Ferro reported annual global sales of USD 959 million in
2020. Ferro  is a  global supplier  of:  (i)  functional coatings  for  glass,  metal,  ceramic and
other substrates, and  (ii) colour solutions in the form of pigments and colorants for a broad
range of industries and  applications. Ferro products are  mainly sold  into  the  building and
construction,   automotive,    industrial   products,   household   furnishings   and   appliance
industries.

Chromaflo  is a US company, headquartered in Ashtabula, Ohio, USA. It is a global supplier
of colourants and chemical pigment dispersions.
