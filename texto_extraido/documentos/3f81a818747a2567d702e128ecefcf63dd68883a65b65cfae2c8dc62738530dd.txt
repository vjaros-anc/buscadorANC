M.10638 ALD / LEASEPLAN

                          SECTION 1.2

                           Description of the concentration

On  5  October  2022,  the  European  Commission  received  notification  of  a  proposed
concentration pursuant to Article 4 of the Merger Regulation by which ALD (France) acquires
within the meaning of Article 3(1)(b) of the Merger Regulation sole control over LP Group
B.V.(the Netherlands), by the way of purchase of shares  in exchange for a combination of
cash and shares of ALD.

ALD  is  the  holding company of the ALD group (“ALD”), a France-headquartered group
providing  mobility  solutions,  mainly  vehicle  leasing and  related management  services.  It  is
held at 79,82% and solely controlled by Société Générale S.A (France), the holding company
of the Société Générale group, a banking and financial services group headquartered in Paris.

LP Group B.V.  is  the  holding company of LeasePlan Corporation N.V (“LeasePlan”), a
Netherlands-based   fleet  management  and   driver   mobility  company.  LeasePlan’s  main
business  is in the sector of vehicle  leasing and related management services.

The  main markets  involved  are  the markets  for  fleet  leasing and management  services  in
which both ALD and LeasePlan are active in several EEA Member States.
