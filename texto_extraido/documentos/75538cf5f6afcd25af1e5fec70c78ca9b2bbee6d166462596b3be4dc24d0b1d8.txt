M.11253 – SAFRAN  / PART OF COLLINS AEROSPACE’s ACTUATION AND
FLIGHT CONTROL ACTIVITIES

                         SECTION 1.2

                           Description of the concentration


This notification concerns the following undertakings:
 ‒   Safran USA Inc. (“Safran USA”), United States, ultimately controlled by Safran S.A.
      (together with its subsidiaries “Safran”), France; and
 ‒   Part of the actuation business of Collins Aerospace, United States, ultimately controlled
     by RTX Corporation (“RTX”).
Safran acquires within the meaning of Article 3(1), point (b) of the Merger Regulation sole
control of part of the actuation business of Collins Aerospace.
The concentration is accomplished pursuant to a Share and Asset Purchase Agreement executed
on 20 July 2023 according to which Safran will acquire the equity of the in-perimeter legal
entities and relevant assets that are part of Collins Aerospace’s actuation business.
The business activities of the undertakings concerned are:
 ‒   For Safran: Safran is a French-registered company listed on the Paris stock exchange
     focusing on three main areas: (i) aerospace propulsion; (ii) aircraft equipment, defence,
     and aerospace systems; and (iii) aircraft interiors. Safran covers the entire lifecycle of
      engines, systems, and equipment for civil and military fixed and rotary-wing aircraft.
 ‒   For the Target: the Target is part of Collins Aerospace’s actuation business. Collins
     Aerospace, a business unit of RTX, provides advanced aerospace and defence products
     and aftermarket service solutions. RTX (formerly Raytheon Technologies, and previously
    known as United Technologies Corporation)  is an aerospace and defence company,
     headquartered in Arlington, Virginia, and listed on NYSE.
