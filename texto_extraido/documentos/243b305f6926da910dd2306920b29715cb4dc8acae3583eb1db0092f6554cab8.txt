(44)     First, Keysight has highlighted the following long-term development plan arising
       from the acquisition of the Divestment Business:
          (a)  The acquisition of the OSG Divestment Business will provide Keysight with
             a suite of software solutions for optical systems design, analysis, simulation,
            and virtual prototyping and a seasoned team with deep industry expertise.
             This will broaden Keysight’s software portfolio and will build on its core
               positions EDA and physics-based computer-aided engineering capabilities.
         (b)  The acquisition of the PCA Divestment Business  will provide a new

2.3.    Absence of prima facie competition problems or implementation risks

(51)    According  to  paragraph  18(c)  of  the Commitments,  the  acquisition  of  the
        Divestment Business by the purchaser must neither be likely to create, in light of
         the information available to the Commission, prima facie competition concerns nor
        give rise to a risk that the implementation of the Commitments will be delayed.
       Based on the information provided by Keysight and the Trustee, the Commission
        considers that these criteria are fulfilled by Keysight.

2.3.1.   Absence of prima facie competition concerns

(52)    The Parties have submitted that the acquisition of the Divestment Businesses by
        Keysight will not give rise to competition concerns.47

(53)    The acquisition of the Divestment Business by Keysight will not prima facie give
          rise to competition concerns.

2.3.1.1. OSG Divestment Business

(54)    Keysight is not active in the field of optics software, where the OSG Divestment
        Business offers its Code V, LightTools, and LucidShape solutions. There is also no
        overlap between Keysight and the OSG Divestment Business in the  field of
        photonics software.48

(55)    The Trustee reported that the acquisition of the Divestment Business by Keysight
        does not appear to raise any apparent competition concerns because Keysight and
         the OSG Divestment Business operate in different segments with no directly
        competing product offerings.49 The Parties also submit, without being contradicted
       by the Trustee, that Keysight’s market position in any market which may be
        neighbouring  or  in a  vertical  relationship with markets  in which  the OSG
        Divestment Business operates would not be sufficiently appreciable to give rise to
       any vertical or conglomerate competitive concerns.50

(56)    The Commission therefore considers that the acquisition of the OSG Divestment
        Business by Keysight would not be likely to create prima facie competition
        concerns.

          this criterion is fulfilled by Keysight.

(67)     First, the Trustee has reported that Keysight is active in this EDA sector.56 This
         position is consistent with the Parties’ submission, which explains that Keysight
         offers Radio Frequency (RF) and Microwave EDA  tools, largely focused on
        monolithic microwave IC (MMIC) applications and RF Module and printed circuit
        board (PCB).57

(68)    Second, Keysight is not active in RTL power consumption analysis and overall
        RTL-related applications.58

(69)     In view of the foregoing and taking into account the Reasoned Opinion submitted
       by the Trustee, the Commission considers that Keysight is active and appears to be
        a credible player in the EDA sector.59
