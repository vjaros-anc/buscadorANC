as Monitoring Trustee in the above-mentioned case.



The  role of the Monitoring Trustee  is to monitor the compliance of the parties with the
Commitments attached to the Commission's Decision of 11 October 2022 and to report to the
Commission thereon.

Third parties who have queries in relation to the Commitments may contact the Trustee through
the above contact details.

The  foregoing  is  without  prejudice  to  the  Commission's  jurisdiction  to  oversee  the
implementation of the Commitments attached to its Decision.
