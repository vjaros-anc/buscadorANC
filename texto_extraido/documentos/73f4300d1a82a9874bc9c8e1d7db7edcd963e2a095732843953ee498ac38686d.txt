M.10431 – ALI GROUP / WELBILT

                         SECTION 1.2

                           Description of the concentration


(1)   On 28  April 2022, the European Commission received a notification of a proposed
       concentration pursuant to Article 4 of Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004 ("EUMR") by
       which  Ali Holding S.r.l., Italy ("Ali Group") intends to acquire sole control within the
      meaning of Article 3(1)(b) EUMR over Welbilt, Inc., USA ("Welbilt"), a company listed on
        the New York Stock Exchange.

(2)    The business activities of the undertakings concerned are:

         Ali Group: commercial and institutional foodservice equipment.

        Welbilt: commercial and institutional foodservice equipment.
