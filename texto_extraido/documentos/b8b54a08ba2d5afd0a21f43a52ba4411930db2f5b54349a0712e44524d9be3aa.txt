M.10860 - ADVENT / GFK

                         SECTION 1.2

                           Description of the concentration


On 20 March 2023, the Commission received notification of the proposed acquisition within
the meaning of Article 3(1)(b) of Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004 whereby, pursuant to
an Equity Purchase Agreement dated 1 July 2022, AI PAVE Dutchco I B.V. (Advent Topco),
which indirectly controls NielsenIQ, is proposing to acquire sole control of GfK SE by way of
an acquisition of shares.

Advent Topco is established in the Netherlands. It is indirectly solely controlled by Advent, a
private equity investor based in the USA.

NielsenIQ is headquartered in the USA and is active in the provision of market research
services. GfK is headquartered in Germany and is also active in the provision of market
research services. The Parties are active worldwide, including in the EEA, the USA and the
Asia-Pacific (APAC) region.
