                   OPINION

                   of the ADVISORY COMMITTEE on MERGERS
                        at its meeting on 01 February 2024

                         concerning a draft decision

          in case M.10149 - KOREAN AIR LINES / ASIANA AIRLINES


                          RAPPORTEUR: ITALY


Concentration

1.    The Advisory Committee (13 Member States) agrees with the Commission that the
        notified transaction constitutes a concentration within the meaning of Article 3(1)(b)
       of the Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004 of 20.1.2004 on the control of
       concentrations between undertakings (the "Merger Regulation")1.

2.    The Advisory Committee (13 Member States) agrees with the Commission that the
        notified operation has a Union dimension pursuant to Article 1(2) of the Merger
       Regulation.

Market definition

Product market definition

3.    The Advisory Committee (13 Member States) agrees with the conclusions reached
      by the Commission in the draft Decision in relation to the definition of the following
       relevant product markets for:

            a)  the supply of passenger  air transport applying an Origin & destination
            (‘O&D’) approach (i.e. potential further segmentation is left open).
          ✓ on “Overlap Routes” between the EEA and Seoul, flights to Incheon
                        airport (ICN), El Prat airport (BCN), Frankfurt am Main (FRA) and
               Rome Fiumicino airport (FCO), each fall into a separate market;
          ✓   it can be left open whether Paris-Charles de Gaulle airport (CDG) and
                      Paris-Orly airport (ORY) are substitutable for flights to Seoul, but
             CDG and Beauvais  airport (BVA) are not close substitutes and
                      therefore fall into separate markets for flights to Seoul;
          ✓  a potential segmentation between direct and indirect flights for the
                     long-haul routes can be  left open, taking into account that direct
                         flights compete more closely with other direct  flights than with
                        indirect flights;

           b) The supply of cargo air transport constitutes a separate product market from
               other modes of transport (i.e. unidirectional from Europe to South Korea and
               vice-versa, including all type of planes but excluding door-to-door services).
          ✓  airport-to-airport  air  cargo  services and  door-to-door  air  cargo
                      services constitute separate product markets.
          ✓  There are no separate product markets for direct and transhipment
                          flights, taking into account that direct flights compete more closely
                    with other direct flights than with transhipment flights.
          ✓  Substitution between scheduled  / regular flights and unscheduled  /
                       charter flights is left open.

Geographic market definition

4.    The Advisory Committee (13 Member States) agrees with the conclusions reached
      by the Commission in the draft Decision in relation to the definition of the relevant
       geographic market for the following product markets for:

            a)  passenger  air transport services  (i.e. between the EEA and South on 4
             Overlapping Routes BCN-ICN, CDG-ICN, FRA-ICN and FCO-ICN).

           b)  cargo air transport  (i.e. unidirectional from Europe – EEA, the UK and
              Switzerland to South Korea and from South Korea to Europe).

Competitive assessment

5.    The Advisory Committee (13 Member  States) agrees with  the Commission’s
       assessment that the notified transaction is likely to significantly impede effective
       competition, as a  result of horizontal non-coordinated effects arising from the
       combination of KOREAN AIR and ASIANA passenger air transport operations by
      removing important competitive constraints on the passenger overlap routes (i.e.
      BCN-ICN, CDG-ICN, FRA-ICN and FCO-ICN.

6.    The Advisory Committee (13 Member  States) agrees with  the Commission’s
       assessment that the notified transaction is likely to significantly impede effective
       competition, as a  result of horizontal non-coordinated effects arising from the
       combination KOREAN AIR and ASIANA cargo  air  transport  operations by
      removing important competitive constraints on the route Europe-South Korea and
       the route South Korea-Europe.

Commitments

7.    The Advisory Committee (13 Member  States) agrees with  the Commission’s
       conclusion that the Passenger Commitments, as offered by the Notifying Party on
       9th of January 2024 remove concerns as to horizontal non-coordinated effects
        arising from the combination of KOREAN AIR and ASIANA passengers  air
        transport operations.

             a.  The Advisory Committee (13 Member States) agrees with the Commission’s
              conclusion that the identification of the Passenger Remedy Taker and making
               the closing of the Transaction conditional to the Passenger Remedy Taker’s
                starting  of  operations on  all  Passenger Remedy Routes  increases  the
               likelihood of timely and sufficient entry on the Passenger Remedy Routes.”

8.    The Advisory Committee (13 Member  States) agrees with  the Commission’s
       conclusion that the Cargo Commitments, as offered by the Notifying Party on 9th of
       January 2024 remove concerns as to horizontal non-coordinated effects arising from
       the combination of KOREAN AIR and ASIANA cargo air transport operations.

9.    The Advisory Committee (13 Member States) agrees with the Commission that,
       subject to the full compliance with the final commitments offered by the Notifying
       Party on 9 January 2024, the notified transaction is not likely to significantly impede
        effective competition in the internal market or in a substantial part of it.

Compatibility with the internal market and the Agreement on the European Economic
Area

10.   The Advisory Committee (13 Member States) agrees with the Commission that the
        notified concentration must therefore be declared compatible with the internal
       market and the Agreement on the European Economic Area2 in accordance with
        Article 2(2) and Article 8(2) of the Merger Regulation and Article 57 of the
      Agreement on the European Economic Area.
