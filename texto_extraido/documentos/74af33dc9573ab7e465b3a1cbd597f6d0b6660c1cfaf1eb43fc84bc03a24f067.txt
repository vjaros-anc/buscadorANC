         Approval of Stadsbader NV as purchaser of EQOS Energie Belgium SA
           following your letter of 26 November 2024 and the Trustee’s opinion of 28
         November 2024


1.     FACTS AND PROCEDURE

(1)    By decision of 16 October 2024 (the ‘Decision’) based on Article 6(1)(b) in
        connection with Article 6(2) of Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004 (the
        ‘Merger Regulation’)1, the Commission declared the operation by which Eiffage
       Energy Systèmes – Participations SAS (‘EES’, France), ultimately controlled by
        Eiffage S.A. (‘Eiffage’, France) acquired within the meaning of Article 3(1)(b) of
         the Merger Regulation, sole control of EQOS Energie Deutschland GmbH
       (‘EQOS’, Germany) and  its subsidiaries, compatible with the internal market
         subject to conditions and obligations (the ‘Commitments’). Eiffage and EQOS are
         together referred to as the ‘Parties’.

(2)     The Commitments require the divestment of EQOS Energie Belgium SA (‘EQOS
        Belgium’ or the ‘Divestment Business’, Belgium), a legal entity wholly owned by
      EQOS, which is active in the installation and maintenance of catenaries and
        overhead contact lines and tracks works in Belgium.
