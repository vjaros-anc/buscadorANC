               EUROPEAN COMMISSION



                                 Hearing Officer for competition proceedings

                                                    Brussels, 2 February 2024


             Final Report of the Hearing Officer1

     Case M.10149 – Korean Air Lines / Asiana Airlines


   On 13 January 2023, the European Commission received a notification of a proposed
     concentration pursuant to Article 4 of Council Regulation (EC) No 139/20042 (the
    ‘Merger Regulation’) by which Korean Air Lines Co. Ltd., controlled by Hanjin KAL
     Co., Ltd, (together ‘Korean Air’ or ‘the Notifying Party’) would acquire, within the
    meaning of Article 3(1)(b) of the Merger Regulation, sole control of Asiana Airlines
     Inc. (‘Asiana’) (together the ‘Parties’), by way of purchase of shares (the ‘Proposed
     Transaction’).

   By decision of 17 February 2023, the Commission raised serious doubts as to the
     compatibility of the Proposed Transaction with the internal market and the EEA
    Agreement3 and decided to initiate proceedings pursuant to Article 6(1)(c) of the
    Merger Regulation.

   On 28 February 2023, the Notifying Party submitted written comments on the
    Commission’s decision of 17 February 2023.

   On 6 March 2023, upon the Notifying Party’s request, and in accordance with Article
     10(3), second subparagraph, of the Merger Regulation, the deadline for the Phase II
    review was extended by 20 working days.

   On 17 May 2023, the Commission adopted a statement of objections (‘SO’) addressed
     to the Notifying Party, which was formally notified to the Notifying Party on the same
     day. Per standard procedure, Asiana was informed that an SO had been adopted. The
     Notifying Party was granted an initial deadline of 2 June 2023 to submit its observations
    on the SO.

   On 22 and 26 May, 8 August, 11, 20 December 2023, 23 January and 1 February 2024,
     the Commission gave access to the file to the Notifying Party. I have not received any
    complaint or request from the Parties regarding access to the file.

   On 25 May 2023, the Notifying Party requested an extension to reply to the SO, until
    7 June 2023. On 26 May 2023, the Commission extended the deadline for the SO reply
     to 6 June 2023.

   On 2 June 2023, the Notifying Party requested an extension to reply to the SO, until
    Wednesday 7 June 2023 included. On the same day, the Commission extended the
     deadline for the SO reply to 7 June 2023.

    The Parties submitted their observations on the SO on 7 June 2023. The Parties did not
     request a hearing pursuant to Article 14 of Regulation (EC) No 802/2004.4

   On 28 March and 26 May 2023, I admitted two entities as interested third persons in
     the present case. On 6 June 2023 these interested third persons received a non-
     confidential version of the SO with a deadline for their comments. Both third persons
     submitted comments on the non-confidential version of the SO, respectively on 9 and
    14 June 2023.

   On 28 June 2023, the Commission adopted a decision pursuant to Article 11(3) of the
    Merger Regulation  (the  ‘Article 11(3)  Decision’). The  Article  11(3) Decision
    compelled Korean Air to submit a complete response to a previous request for
     information (‘RFI’) sent by the Commission and had the effect of suspending the time
     limits referred to in the first subparagraph of Article 10(3) of the Merger Regulation.
    The Notifying Party complied with the Article 11(3) Decision on 5 December 2023.
    The applicable time limit was therefore suspended from 23 June 2023 to 5 December
    2023.

   On 8 August 2023, a letter of facts (‘LoF’) was issued. The LoF informed the Parties
    about additional factual elements in support of the objections set out in the SO. DG
    Competition set a time limit to respond until 23 August 2023. On 23 August 2023, the
     Notifying Party commented on the LoF.

   On 3 November 2023, the Notifying Party submitted a formal commitment package in
     order to address the identified competition concerns. On the basis of its assessment, the
    Commission concluded that these commitments would not solve the competition
    concerns identified by the Commission.

   On 5 December 2023, the Notifying Party submitted revised commitments to the
    Commission (the ‘Revised Commitments’). The Commission launched a market test
    on the Revised Commitments on 6 December 2023. On 9 January 2024 the Notifying
     Party submitted amended commitments (the ‘Final Commitments’).

     In the draft final decision pursuant to Article 8(2) of the Merger Regulation, the
    Commission concludes that the Final Commitments are sufficient to address the
     competition concerns which the Commission had raised regarding the compatibility of
     the Proposed Transaction with the internal market. The draft decision therefore declares
     the Proposed Transaction compatible with the internal market and the functioning of
     the EEA Agreement, subject to full compliance with the Final Commitments.
