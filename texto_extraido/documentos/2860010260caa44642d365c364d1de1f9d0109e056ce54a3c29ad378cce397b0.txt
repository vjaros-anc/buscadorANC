M.12278 – PARAMOUNT SKYDANCE / WARNER BROS DISCOVERY

                         SECTION 1.2

                           Description of the concentration


This notification concerns a concentration pursuant to Article 4 of Council Regulation (EC)
139/2004 of 20 January 2004 on the control of concentrations between undertakings.

Pursuant to the Merger Agreement entered into by the Parties on 27 February 2026, PSKY
will acquire sole control over WBD within the meaning of Article 3(1)(b) EUMR, by way of
an all-cash offer of all outstanding shares of WBD at a price of USD 31.00 per share. The
Transaction  is subject to customary closing conditions, including regulatory clearances.
WBD’s shareholders approved the transaction on 23 April 2026.10

PSKY, a U.S. publicly listed company headquartered in New York,  is a global media,
streaming and entertainment company that creates content and experiences for audiences
worldwide. PSKY’s portfolio includes Paramount Pictures, Paramount Television, CBS, CBS
News, CBS Sports, Nickelodeon, MTV, BET, Comedy Central, Showtime, Paramount+,
Pluto TV and Skydance Media’s animation, interactive/games and sports divisions.

WBD, a U.S. publicly listed company headquartered in New York, is a global media and
entertainment company that creates and distributes a diverse portfolio of content and products
across television, film, streaming and interactive gaming. WBD’s portfolio of brands and
production assets includes, among others, Discovery Channel, CNN, DC Studios, New Line
Cinema, TNT Sports, Eurosport, Max, HBO, WBMPG, WBTVG, and Warner Bros. Games.
