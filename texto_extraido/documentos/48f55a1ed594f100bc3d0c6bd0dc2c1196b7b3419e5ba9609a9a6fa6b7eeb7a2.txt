       MONITORING AND DIVESTITURE TRUSTEE IN CASE

                    M.11956 – UMG / DOWNTOWN


On 13 February 2026 the Commission approved the appointment of:

                 Monitoring Trustee Partners B.V.
                Koninginneweg 215
               1075 CS Amsterdam
               The Netherlands

                  Contact:

                 Jan Jaap Snel, Managing Director, janjaapsnel@mtp.international
                  Astrid Bellon, Director, astridbellon@mtp.international

as Monitoring and Divestiture Trustee in the above-mentioned case.


The role of the Monitoring Trustee is to monitor the compliance of Universal Music
Group N.V. and Downtown Music Holdings LLC with the Commitments attached to the
Commission's Decision of 13 February 2026 and to report to the Commission thereon.

The role of the Divestiture Trustee is to sell the divestment business in line with the
Commitments attached to the Commission's Decision of 13 February 2026.

Third parties who have queries in relation to the Commitments may contact the Trustee
through the above contact details.

The foregoing  is without prejudice to the Commission's jurisdiction to oversee the
implementation of the Commitments attached to its Decision.
