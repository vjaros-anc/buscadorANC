  MONITORING TRUSTEE IN CASE M.10507 – HITACHI RAIL / GROUND
        TRANSPORTATION SYSTEMS BUSINEES OF THALES


On 6 November 2023 the Commission approved the appointment of:

                 Monitoring Trustee Partners B.V.
                Koninginneweg 215
               1075 CS Amsterdam
               The Netherlands

                  Contact: Jan Jaap Snel
                  janjaapsnel@mtp.international

as Monitoring Trustee in the above-mentioned case.


The role of the Monitoring Trustee is to monitor the compliance of Hitachi Rail Ltd. with
the Commitments attached to the Commission's Decision of 30 October 2023 and to
report to the Commission thereon.

Third parties who have queries in relation to the Commitments may contact the Trustee
through the above contact details.

The foregoing  is without prejudice to the Commission's jurisdiction to oversee the
implementation of the Commitments attached to its Decision.
