M.11974 - VANDEMOORTELE / DELIFRANCE

                          SECTION 1.2

                            Description of the concentration

Vandemoortele (via VAMIX NV) will acquire sole control within the meaning of Article 3 (1),
point (a) of the Merger Regulation over Délifrance by way of purchase of shares. The Transaction
will create a European-based global market player with combined bakery product revenues of EUR
2.4 billion, providing a solid basis for further international expansion, accelerating ambitious
sustainability plans and enhanced competitiveness.

This notification concerns the following undertakings:
    1.  Vandemoortele Group ("Vandemoortele"), a 100% family-owned global manufacturer
      and supplier of food products headquartered in Ghent, Belgium; and

    2.  Délifrance S.A. ("Délifrance"), a manufacturer and supplier of Frozen Bakery Products
       headquartered in Ivry-Sur-Seine, France, controlled by Vivescia Group.

The business activities of the undertakings concerned are:

    1.  For Vandemoortele: The manufacture and supply of plant-based food solutions, including
         fats, sauces, margarines and oils, as well as frozen bakery products; and

    2.  For Délifrance: The manufacture and supply of frozen bakery products (and some ambient
      and prepackaged consumer products).
